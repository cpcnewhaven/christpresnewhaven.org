CPC College Ministry
Kevin Nelson, pastor of university ministries

CPC’s college ministry is a collection of students from wide-ranging backgrounds gathered under the banner of Jesus Christ. Mostly made up of Yale students, the college ministry also includes students from other area universities. CPC supports several on-campus ministries at Yale, including Reformed University Fellowship, the campus ministry of the Presbyterian Church in America.

CPC seeks to help students love Christ, love the church, and love the campus life to which they are called. Wanting students to be involved in deeply satisfying relationships in the church, we offer a mentoring program in which college students mentor high schoolers, and another in which college students are mentored by grad students. (The dates for these programs in 2007-2008 are below.) Because we love the campus, we not only send campus ministers to the campus but also welcome students from the campus to be part of the CPC College Ministry Team, a group of university students which helps the church connect to students even while themselves being trained and mentored by the pastors and staff of CPC. Above all, we invite students to take part in the fullness of Christ through CPC’s rich, communal Sunday morning worship. Students, please honor us with your presence and unique gifts!
