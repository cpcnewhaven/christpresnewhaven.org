CPC College
The CPC college ministry, made up of students from several area colleges, is driven by CPC’s commitment to the Scriptures, the Reformed doctrines of grace, God-centered covenantal worship, cultural engagement, and a high view of Christian calling. How do college students live out these commitments at CPC?

  1. Worship in the community of faith. College students at CPC worship alongside others of various ages and backgrounds, together lifting up the covenant-keeping God, hearing and confessing the gospel of Jesus, and seeking transformation through His Word and Spirit.
  2. CPC College Fellowship. Beginning Fall of 2006, CPC now proves that, contrary to popular opinion, there is such a thing as a free lunch! On Sundays after the second service (12:15-1:15), college students gather in the fellowship hall for lunch and fellowship, to learn from each other and interact over the Book of Proverbs. Our theme for the year is “Living the Life,” focused on helping college students work out the practical implications of following Christ on campus.
    Rides are available from Yale’s campus every Sunday – meet at Phelps Gate at 10:15
  3. CPC Student Partnership. Many CPC college students take part in a mentoring program focused on high school students within the congregation. High school students are paired with a college mentor of the same sex and (typically) the same year in school. All college mentors and their high school friends meet together once a month to connect with one another and interact with biblical teaching. College students have the opportunity to give brief messages and testimonies, lead social events, and engage high school students in prayer and discussion.
  4. CPC Internship. The internship is for those students seeking greater equipping and preparation for a life of service to Christ, in whatever vocation God might call them to. Interns serve under the mentorship of the college pastor in a particular field of ministry, while pursuing a study and training plan designed to give them the tools for leadership in the church both during college and beyond.
Are you interested in knowing more about worship at CPC, the College Fellowship, the Student Partnership, or the Internship? Contact Clay Daniel, pastor to college students (cpc.clay@snet.net or 203.676.5378), or Rebekah Caldwell, CPC ministry intern (rebekahcaldwell@mac.com or 901.340.3600).
