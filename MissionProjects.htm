Summer of Love

From July 6th through 14th we have the privilege of hosting and working alongside members of Independent Presbyterian Church of Memphis, Tennessee as we labor together to show Christ’s love in the city of New Haven.  We will be holding a VBS soccer camp in the Hill community.  John Daniels school and Resurrection Lutheran have graciously opened their facilities to us.  We will also be doing significant repair work on the church building.  As the Study Center is 157 years old, there is plenty of work that needs doing to keep it in good condition.  Please join us in prayer for the city of New Haven, for the people involved, and for God’s love to be seen. For more specific requests, click here.  Everyone is invited to come together for dinner, prayer, sharing, and worship every night of the project. We look forward to seeing you there.

Schedule:
Friday Welcome Dinner
Saturday Training, Study Center Repair, Registration/Health Fair, dinner and worship
Sunday Worship, morning and evening, and cookout
Monday Camp and Study Center repair, evening dinner and worship
Tuesday Camp and Study Center repair, evening dinner and worship
Wednesday Camp and Study Center repair and West Haven beach day, evening dinner and Christian faith and practice discussion
Thursday Camp and Study Center repair and Habitat for Humanity building, evening dinner and worship
Friday Camp and Study Center repair and afternoon off, evening dinner and worship
