Study of the Apostles' Creed
This class reflects on two of our five core values -- gospel-centered and confessional -- on a level accessible to everyone.  The class will be going through Michael Horton's book We Believe and will be taught by the session.
 
Church History I
Would you like to know more about the history and theology of the church from the close of the New Testament through the 5th century?  Then take this class!  The class will be taught by Mark Anderson and Junius Johnson

To listen to lectures please visit the sermons download page here.

Syllabus

Jan. 18    History Introduction, Theology Introduction
(social backdrop, transition from NT times, state of the church report [what does it look like, etc.]) (Chadwick, Chapters 1 and 2);Plato, Aristotle, Stoics, Epicureans, etc. (Kelly pp. 14-20)

Jan. 25    History: Roman attitudes towards Christians and Christian responses, martyrdom
(Pliny, Ignatius, Justin Martyr) (Chadwick, Chapters 3 and 4)

Feb. 1    Theology: Irenaeus & the Gnostic challenge
(Kelly pp. 22-28, 35-41, 104-108, 170-174)

Feb. 8    History: Montanus and canonization/growth of Christianity
(Tertullian) (Kelly, Chapters 5 and 6)

Feb. 15    Theology: Modalism and systematic theology
(Sabellius, Origen) (Kelly pp. 119-123, 126-136, 469-474)

Feb.  22    History: Lapsist controversy
(Chadwick, Chapter 7)

Mar. 1    History: Nicea-Constantinople
(Eusebius, Constantine) (Chadwick, Chapters 8 and 9)

Mar. 8    Theology: Nicea-Constantinople

Mar. 15    Theology: Ephesus
