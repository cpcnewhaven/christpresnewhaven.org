Servant Ministry

The Servant Leader Board provides help and support to both the Session and the congregation in the practical aspects of fostering the kind of authentic love among the members of the body of Christ as outlined by Paul in Romans 12. Comprised of church members who each serve for three years, the role of the Servant Leader Board is to express the communion of saints and to develop the grace of Christian service within the larger membership of the church. This includes general oversight and leadership in regards to the other servant leader teams, and recommendations to the session about the organization of the various teams, leaders, church calendar, activities, budget, etc. The SLB meets monthly as a group, frequently together with members of the Session to discuss the overall health and well-being of the church and to provide an exchange of any other related counsel deemed helpful to the functioning of the Board.

The Servant Leader Board oversees these 11 teams.  If you are in need of information or interested in serving on any of these teams, please contact the chairperson listed beside the team name.
