A LETTER TO PARENTS
The General Philosophy and Strategy Of Christian Education for Children in CPC

Welcome to the new year of Christian Education at Christ Presbyterian Church! We look forward to another year of helping you train your child in the Christian faith. We have developed a very thoughtful and comprehensive approach towards providing your children with deep and enduring roots in the Christian faith. Our philosophy emphasizes close parent/church cooperation. Our strategy for "making disciples" seeks to be primarily relational rather than programmatic. Our curriculum represents both a deductive and inductive approach to education that is intended to give our children a lasting and full knowledge of the Christian faith. Our goal, then, is to help our children become faithful disciples of Christ, well prepared to live and defend the gospel into the 21st century. By the time a child leaves the home, they would have been trained in 1) a comprehensive study of the Bible, in all its various periods of redemptive history 2) a systematic study and review of the more essential teachings of the classic/evangelical Christian faith, 3) a precious collection of classic hymns to assist in private and public worship, 4) a practical and personal knowledge of Christ as experienced within the loving community of a Christ-centered Church. A brief outline of our strategy for making Christian disciples of our children is as follows:

Church's Part:

a. Inductive: Classroom Bible Studies

Each Sunday school class studies the Scripture using lessons and curriculums most suited to each age. Children are helped to develop a conviction for the Christian faith that is grounded in the scripture.
b. Deductive: Children's Assembly
The Children's assembly period is devoted to the review and explanation of Westminster Shorter Catechisms together with the learning of hymns and creeds often used in Worship.
c. Regular Pastoral Visitations:
1. Monthly group meeting: The pastor meets with the children during the first assembly time of each month to introduce the catechism questions selected for that month.
2. Annual Individual meetings: The pastor annually meets with each child individually in order to better understand the needs and concerns of each child. An informal assessment is made as to the child's progress toward participating in the Lord's Table.
d. Various special Activities:
1. Summer Vacation Bible School where Christian themes are discussed and modeled within a fun "camp" atmosphere.
2. Terrific Thursdays: Special outings each Thursday of the summer for children and parents
3. Bi-Annual Children's party
e. Preparation for participation in communion:
There is no age requirement for participation in communion. Only a credible profession of faith is required. Among other things, a credible profession of faith requires an aptitude for sober-mindedness such that careful self-examination of ones relation to Christ can be adequately accomplished in preparation for the Lord's table.(1 Cor. 11:28) Once a child is considered ready for such self-examination, (as determined through pastoral consolation with parent and child) the child is provided with a take home study consisting of 15 lessons. Each lesson is completed by the child and reviewed by the parent. The child has two consultations with either the pastor or one of the elders after the 7th and 15th lesson. Upon completion of the take home study, a meeting is scheduled with the session for examination as to a credible profession of faith. (See "Letter to Parents of children seeking admission to the Lord's Table")
*A paper entitled "Admission to the Lord's Table" is intended to help a parent assess a child's readiness for participation in the Lord's table. (Located on Book Table)

Parent's Part:

a. Regular Shorter Catechism Training:

At a pace suitable to each child, parents help their children understand and memorize the answers to the Westminster Shorter Catechisms. Each month, the child is provided with the specific catechisms that are being reviewed in the children's assembly.
b. Weekly Review of Sunday School lessons
Parents are encouraged to go over the Sunday school material with children sometime on Sunday afternoons or evenings. Each child is given a take-home sheet to facilitate parental involvement.
c. Family Worship and Hymns Training:
Parents are encouraged to faithfully gather the family together for family worship where hymns are practiced and enjoyed, scripture is read and prayers are offered to God for spiritual edification and renewal. We believe that families who worship together, grow together and are most able to participate joyfully together in corporate worship.
d. Communion Preparation:
As previously described, the parent is asked to review the child's take home lessons provided by the pastor.
Ending Note: It would be tragic to equate children's programs with children discipleship. This is why our strategy emphasizes close relational involvement between the parent, child and church.
