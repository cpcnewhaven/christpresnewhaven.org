Standard Curriculum and Training in Adult Education
Christ Presbyterian Church


We have developed a very comprehensive strategy toward "making disciples" through "teaching them all things whatsoever that I have commanded." The backbone of this strategy consist of weekly expositional preaching from God's Word. Yet we have also provided several other opportunities for instruction in the Christian faith so as to facilitate Christian growth in the love and knowledge of God. While there are several other teaching opportunities that are provided through CPC that usually reflect some particular interest or topic,* we hope that most people could eventually complete the three levels of systematic Christian instruction as listed below under "core CPC curriculum." Those active in the various forms of leadership in CPC are also provided with that kind of instruction and training especially suitable to a given calling in the church as summarized below under "Leadership training at CPC."
* Some of these include our Christian Faith and Practice series, family group discussions, special seminars, Mid-week Bible study.

Core CPC Curriculum

I. Discovery Class
A five week class aimed at introducing a person to Jesus Christ. Led apologetically through discussion. No preparation or previous Christian experience is necessary.

II. Foundations Class: An Ancient Faith for a Modern People
A 15 session discussion course using the Apostle's Creed. The class concentrates on the "essentials" of the Christian faith aimed at helping a young believer become "rooted and grounded in faith." No preparation necessary.

III. Theology Class
A 28 week systematic study of classic evangelical/reformed theology aimed at training a person in "sound theology" toward becoming a teacher and/or officer at Christ Presbyterian Church. Continuity within the historic reformed tradition will be illustrated from readings which seek to span the duration of church history. The method of this class will consist of directed readings together with class discussion. Each student is expected to put at least 4 hours of reading time into each class.

Leadership training at CPC

I. Quarterly Servant Leadership Training
A monthly gathering of all "key leaders" (team coordinators, teachers, deacons, deacons assistants and elders) for the purpose of training and instruction regarding servant leadership in the church. Time is also allotted for prayer, ministry planning and coordination, training in Evangelism, Bible Interpretation, Delegation and other related issues.

II. Training for Teachers:
All teachers are expected to have theological training as offered in the systematic study of theology offered in our general curriculum. Teachers are further trained in a bi-annual "teachers workshop" whereby principles of teaching and interpretation of the Bible are provided together with time for prayer and general planning.

III. Training for Deacon Leadership:
A seven week class aimed at training candidates for the office of deacon. Topics include the character of a deacon, the theology of a deacon and the role of a deacon in relation to facilitating the communion of saints at CPC. While each candidate will be required to be familiar with all of the shorter catechism questions, together with a basic understanding of their meaning, candidates will be required to memorize 23 of the assigned Shorter Catechism answers.

IV. Training for Elder Leadership:
In addition to having completed theological training comparable to the above class in systematic theology, (28 week class), those aspiring to the offices of elder will receive instruction in the character, and responsibilities of a ruling elder together with a general understanding of the Book of Church Order, PCA in a class lasting seven weeks.
