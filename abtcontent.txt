At Christ Presbyterian Church, New Haven, we desire to be ambitious for
the glory of God by <B>growing</B> in the truth and grace of Jesus Christ,
<B>acting</B> as a faithful witness in the greater New Haven community and world and
humbly <B>trusting</B> in the grace and power of the Holy Spirit in everything we
do and say.  After the pattern of Acts 2:37-47 we ask that God would make us
more and more devoted to learning, worshipping, and one-anothering such
that the people of every nation are welcomed to Christ even as Christ is
given first place in everything.  Through the links on the left you'll find
information about our beliefs, our strategies, and our history as a church.  You may also want to check out "The Window," Christ Presbyterian's
newsletter (both present and past editions are available for viewing - just click on the tab above) and this recent <A HREF="javascript:parent.location='http://www.pcanet.org/mna/multiply/multiply2002/spring2002/spring2002.htm#NewHaven'">article</A> featuring our church.
