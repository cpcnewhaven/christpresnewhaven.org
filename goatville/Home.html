We believe in a big God who is majestic beyond all imagination and yet intimate beyond all love. And we believe that He has only begun to manifest Himself through the Body in New Haven. Jesus promises that by His Spirit we will see “greater things” than the disciples themselves witnessed in his life – greater things! We desire to see this happen in our beloved city.

Seeking the holistic Temple presence of God, we will be wrestling with Scripture alongside those who have gone before us, participating in the Spirit through prayer, and experiencing Christ through one another. In all things, we hope to cling to the overwhelming grace of the gospel in our lives and world. Being in the world and for the world, we believe that our humble community can actually become the place of Christ’s presence on earth. Won’t you join us?
