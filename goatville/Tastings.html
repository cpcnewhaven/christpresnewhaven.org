New Haven Tastings

A concert series featuring the tastes and sounds of New Haven.


The Performers:


Mon Monarch
Mon Monarch is Chuck E. Costa, Eric Tate and Colin Meyer. After a few years making music together they were compelled to give a name to the growing creative collaboration between the three of them. Upon approaching the recording process for their debut album as "Mon Monarch," they conceived a vision for the project to capture an honest record of Chuck's songs with his raw delivery still intact but without losing a very high standard of audio quality in the recording itself. So they holed up in a cabin in upstate NY, having lugged up the highest quality equipment they could beg for, borrow or rent, and set to work.

The result is the band's debut, "Waterproof Matches," which is currently available as a limited digital pre-release. "On first encounter with Mon Monarch's winningly understated debut, you might be forgiven for thinking that the elegant New Haven, Connecticut trio hails from Northern California, such is the bucolic, loving, laid-back, pastoral, softly swooning, indulging warmth of this recording. These are songs of well-penned subtlety. Because the band has a history there is depth, strength and maturity. Consummate musical intelligence, clarity of expression, fun and satisfying nuance and a real knack for simple, but beautifully elegant touches make "Waterproof Matches" an aurally pleasing album." (Jivewired.com)

The band has decided to SHARE the entire album to all of their fans as well as anyone who comes to a live performance. They are releasing it in a spirit of appreciation to the fans who support them and to establish a true and honest connection with anyone and everyone who truly feels the value of what they are doing.

Chuck E. Costa has just recently been named the official Connecticut State Troubadour for the two year 2011-2012 term!

Lamont Hiebert (of Ten Shekel Shirt)
Lamont Hiebert is a Love146 Co-Founder and the Director of U.S. Prevention. After helping establish the programmatic direction for the organization from 2002-2006 as the Executive Director, Lamont's current focus is on preventing child sex trafficking in the U.S.A. Lamont is also a successful singer/songwriter, most notably as the front man for the band Ten Shekel Shirt. His well known songs include Fragile, Ocean, and Spark. He has been the musical guest of GQ Magazine, the NBA, International Justice Mission and has performed at social action events with Dave Matthews Band, John Legend and Natasha Bedingfield. As a seasoned Love146 spokesperson and performing artist, Lamont powerfully communicates the challenges and successes of the modern-day abolitionist movement through lectures, stories and songs.

Jonny Rodgers
Jonny Rodgers writes beautiful, quirky songs using guitars, tuned wine glasses, electronics and a unique chamber orchestra. He is also a composer/producer, writing chamber, orchestral and choral music for concert and for film.

His formative musical years were spent writing, touring and recording with his brother Steve Rodgers in the indie band Mighty Purple.

With the new millennium Jonny began to combine disparate influences and a compositional sensibility into a quirky and beautiful sound all his own.  With his indie background as a springboard, Jonny immersed himself in the world of composed music.  He began studying with Yale composer Padma Newsome (Clogs, The National) and played bass for NYC’s classical-crossover band Awry (now My Brightest Diamond). Jonny began collaborating with many talented musicians from the world of Classical and New Music.  Within the span of a year he had written the material for The Aviary, and began performing the entire album as a live show with a 9-piece chamber orchestra featuring highly unusual elements, such as Tuned Wine Glasses and Steel Drum. His newest material prominently features Tuned Wine Glasses, Guitars and Electronics with live loops used in unexpected ways.
