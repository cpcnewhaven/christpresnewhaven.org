November 2001

 

Dear Friend:

 

We would like to tell you about an exciting new venture that has been started to serve the needs of the Greater New Haven community.  Hope for New Haven, Inc. is a faith-based, non-profit organization that is a collaborative project between Christ Presbyterian Church of New Haven, St. John’s Episcopal Church, and Trinity Baptist Church. Our mission is to respond to the physical, spiritual, developmental and vocational needs in the lives of individuals and families in the Greater New Haven area by providing high quality childcare and other important services such as Christian counseling, student tutoring, and music education.  We hope that you will partner with us to invest in the lives of our community’s children and families.

 

Our strategy is to meet the most pressing needs in our community, beginning with childcare. Our research confirms the need for these services. In New Haven last year there was a shortage of over 1,500 childcare slots. In the same year, the demand for multiple services went up more than 61%. Out of Connecticut’s 169 cities and towns, New Haven is second only to Hartford in calls to Info Line (a statewide resource and referral line) for resources and assistance. Childcare is the number one request and Christian counseling, marriage and individual therapy ranked number five. Almost half of requests for counseling were seeking services that could be provided within a Christian or religious framework. We believe our programs are unique because they are based on community request to fill a present gap in services and are being developed and delivered through a collaboration of area churches and community members.

 

You are receiving this letter because someone from Hope for New Haven, Inc. or one of the three collaborating churches recommended you to us.  We are excited about your potential interest in our programs and are including relevant materials for your review. With your financial support, Hope for New Haven’s goals for FY 2001- 2002 are: 1) to create 28 new child care slots for families in the city of New Haven, with 9 sliding scale slots for families in need of financial assistance; 2) professional counseling services from a Christian perspective; 3) student tutoring in the New Haven area; and 4) formal music education and lessons for inner city children.  We have already begun the student tutoring programs by our support of STEP, which serves children in the Dixwell neighborhood.   And last spring we initiated our music education program, also serving children in the Dixwell area.  We plan on beginning our counseling and daycare/early childhood education programs after the first of the year.  In order to successfully launch all of these programs, we estimate that we will need to raise approximately $85,000 from both granting agencies and individual contributors. 

 

Would you please consider partnering with us in this exciting venture by making a financial contribution to Hope for New Haven, Inc.?  Hope for New Haven, Inc. is a 501(c)(3) organization.  Therefore, all contributions are tax-exempt to the extent allowed by law.  If your employer has a program for matching employee charitable contributions, we would encourage you to notify your employer of your contribution so that Hope for New Haven, Inc.  might benefit from these matching funds.  We would be happy to provide any additional information that your employer may require regarding in order for Hope for New Haven to be eligible for those matching funds.

 

Thank you for your time and interest. If you wish to contact us, please do not hesitate to call (203) 865-HOPE from 9am-5pm.
