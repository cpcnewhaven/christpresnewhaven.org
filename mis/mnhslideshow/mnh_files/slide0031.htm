Building Project Status
•August 1997: Purchased 135 Whitney Ave. consisting of a Historic Gothic Revival Style Building and undeveloped land for future development.
•January 2000: Architects were retained and work on design was initiated.
•May 2000: Gained city approval to build after a considerable contest.
•August 2000: Retain Holt Construction Co. as the Construction Manager
•October 2000: Most of construction documents are complete and put out for  bid and the initial building permit is pulled for site and foundation work. 
•November 2000: “Guaranteed Maximum Price” came in at 2 million plus 100k “furniture.”
