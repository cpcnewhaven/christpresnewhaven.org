"The Strategic Plan"
The Strategic Plan

An ambitious plan for
a multi-cultural
Church Planting Movement
Year 1: Hire an African-American church planter targeting a multi-cultural/ African-American community.
Year 2: A second service in new facility.
Year 3: Hire a Latino-American church planter targeting a multi-cultural/Latino-American community.
Year 5: Hire a Anglo-American church planter targeting a town in Southern Connecticut.

An ambitious plan
for reaching university students 
through a local affiliation with
Reformed University Ministries
Presently reaching Yale University with RUM minister, Rev.Tom Morrison
Plans to reach Southern Connecticut State University, University of  New Haven, Quinnipiac University, Albertus Magnus College.

An ambitious plan for
faith based services of mercy
 under a subsidiary, 501c(3)
 Hope for New Haven
A Christian daycare center and family services targeting needy families in New Haven.
A tutorial and mentoring program for inner city youth of New Haven.
Christian counseling services for people of Southern Connecticut.
Other holistic ministries of mercy with collaborating churches targeting the needs of New Haven.

An ambitious plan
for a “hub” facility
Perfectly located in the historic district of downtown New Haven and adjacent to Yale University.
The charm of a historic, 1850’s Gothic Revival style study center
The functionality of a newly built worship center, daycare center and office suite for Hope for New Haven

The Scope of the
Building Project
To restore the historic portion of the existing building and use as a study center and church offices. (completed)
To renovate 2,000 square feet in basement of existing study center for additional program space. (in process)
To design and construct a new, multi-story facility of about 10,000 square feet to house a worship space, a day care facility and offices for Hope for New Haven.

Building Project Status
August 1997: Purchased 135 Whitney Ave. consisting of a Historic Gothic Revival Style Building and undeveloped land for future development.
January 2000: Architects were retained and work on design was initiated.
May 2000: Gained city approval to build after a considerable contest.
August 2000: Retain Holt Construction Co. as the Construction Manager
October 2000: Most of construction documents are complete and put out for  bid and the initial building permit is pulled for site and foundation work.
November 2000: “Guaranteed Maximum Price” came in at 2 million plus 100k “furniture.”
