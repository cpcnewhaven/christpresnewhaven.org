The Scope of the
Building Project
• To restore the historic portion of the existing building and
use as a study center and church offices. (completed)
• To renovate 2,000 square feet in basement of existing
study center for additional program space. (in process)
• To design and construct a new, multi-story facility of about
10,000 square feet to house a worship space, a day care
facility and offices for Hope for New Haven.
