May 16, 2003

 

Dear Supporters and Friends of CPC:

 

A recent Washington Post article describes how today’s spirituality on college campuses is both on the rise and in flux (May 4, 2003, “On Campus, Spiritual Groups Witness a Cultural Conversion”).  Religious commitment is described as increasingly pluralistic in culturally unexpected ways.   As illustrated by two Yale roommates, the article further describes a “cultural conversion” of sorts where the cultural “west” is becoming increasingly eastern in spirituality, even as the cultural “east” is becoming increasingly western in spirituality.   In sum, there is perhaps an unprecedented interest in spirituality that is coupled with an unprecedented openness to a plurality of religious expression.  

 

Here at Yale, Chaplain Jerry Streets observes how Yale students are remarkably interested in having a sense of transcendence… [just as] Yale today is more religiously pluralistic than it ever was in the past.  This is borne out in a recent study of the religious climate at Yale where over 61% of students are said to be active in some form of spirituality and 29% of students stated that spirituality was MORE important in their lives while in college than when they were at home.  And yet, over 60% are active in a religious tradition that is different than when they were growing up and 77% agreed that their spirituality does not depend on being involved in a religious organization!

 

This brief “snapshot” of campus spirituality and Yale University perfectly illustrates both the opportunity and challenge for the gospel that we have in New Haven!   Students are more interested in “spirituality” than ever, even as they are more reluctant to participate in “organized religion” than ever!   In such a context, the gospel can flourish as by it’s own inherent power, but it will need to do so within the competing milieu of campus spirituality—it will need to be personal, present, and engaging!   And this is exactly what excites me about our vision for a Reformed Christian Fellowship (RCF) at Yale), as affiliated with Reformed University Fellowship nationally (RUF) and especially the recent invitation to Clay and Sara Daniel to join us in this ministry!    

 

Having spent a great deal of time getting to know Clay, we believe that Clay’s gifts and training is a perfect fit for this opportunity.  Holding fast to the classic confession of the evangelical faith and a conviction for the church as an essential element of the gospel, Clay is well suited toward establishing a gospel presence on the campus of Yale University!  (Clay completed his undergrad training at Harvard, and his Seminary training at Dallas Theological Seminary while completing his pastoral internship at Park Cities Presbyterian Church.)   Even as more and more students have been attending CPC sense our transition into our new worship facility adjacent to campus, we believe that having a vibrant and on campus ministry is essential to reaching the “world” that has assembled in a place like Yale.  The complementary aspects of an on-campus AND church based ministry is we believe the ideal approach to reaching the University. And without a doubt, to have an impact at a place like Yale is to have an impact in the future board rooms, governments, classrooms and market places all over the world!

 

In so far as this mission is to reach the unchurched, and in so far as we are doing this in the frontier context of New England—the resources that are needed will largely come from folks like you who will partner with us in this exciting opportunity. More specifically, while Clay and Sara are raising a significant percentage of the support they will need to join us hopefully by Fall, with your help, I am committed to raising at least 13k annual outside support to supplement the needed RCF total budget of 73k.  Perhaps you have supported our ministry before, or perhaps you are for the first time being approached by us for support.  But this would be an excellent time to join us in ministry if God has put it in your heart to do so.   You may use the enclosed envelop for your convenience to send whatever you can and to communicate to us your interest for the future of this ministry.   Unlike in the recent past when we have been without a campus minister, we plan to send you monthly reports/updates concerning our ministry at Yale starting when Clay can report to “duty.” If I can be of further assistance in helping you to discern your involvement in this ministry, please don’t hesitate to call or write.  (e-mail: cpc.preston@snet.net)

 

Thank you so much for considering this strategic ministry by means of a financial and prayer partnership with RCF and CPC!  

 

 

I am affectionately yours in Christ,

 

 

Preston Graham Jr.

Sr. Pastor, Christ Presbyterian Church, New Haven
