<P ALIGN=left>
<B><FONT COLOR=blue>Mission to New Haven</FONT></B> summarizes Christ
Presbyterian Church's vision for reaching the communities of greater New
Haven with the Gospel of Jesus Christ.  At the very core of our mission is to
<I>be</I> "missional" rather than merely to "do" missions.  Our aim is to
foster a culture of invitation and grace in worship, fellowship, small group
Bible Studies, and informal gatherings.  Our mission strategy includes three
major projects: Hope For New Haven, a multi-cultural and
multi-congregational approach to church growth, and Reformed University
Ministries which targets the area universities of New Haven.  For more
information, please view the slide show to the left which elaborates on the
specifics of our mission.  Please also visit the <!--local <A HREF="javascript:parent.location='http://www.yale.edu/rcf/'">RCF</A> (Reformed
