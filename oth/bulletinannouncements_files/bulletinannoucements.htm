Sign-up for Youth Retreat:

PARENTS and ALL 9TH- 12TH GRADERS – please see information on the foyer table for the upcoming Spring Youth Retreat – April 8th – 10th, 2005.  The retreat will be held at Lakeside Christian Camp & Conference Center – Pittsfield, MA.  This is an awesome chance to fellowship with other Christian youth from all over the Southern New England Presbytery of the PCA, be encouraged & challenged in your Christian walk, & have a blast at the same time!  Josh Strecker & Kyle Christopher will lead worship & Bo White (Teaching elder & youth pastor at Twin Oaks Presbyterian Church in St. Louis) will be our speaker.  By all accounts Bo is an awesome speaker & well worth going to hear even if it wasn’t for all the games, cool people (us), great food & other fun stuff.   Registration forms &  fee of $95are due to Ilona by March 7th.
