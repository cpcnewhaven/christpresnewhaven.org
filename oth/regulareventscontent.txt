<p align=center style='text-align:center'><b><u><span style='font-size:13.5pt;
color:blue'>Church Wide Prayer </span></u></b></p>

<p>Congregational prayer with Elders is held every third Wednesday at <st1:time
Minute="30" Hour="18">6:30 pm</st1:time> at CPC. (Please refer to the latest <a
href="javascript:parent.location='bulletinannouncements.htm'">Bulletin
Announcements</a> for updated information.) </p>

<p>Contact our Music Director: Daniel Kellogg at 777-6960 or email: <a
href="mailto:Daniel.Kellogg@yale.edu">Daniel.Kellogg@yale.edu</a> if you are
interested in any of the below. </p>
