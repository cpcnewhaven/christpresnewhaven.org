College Ministry at CPC

 

What do we care about?

 

            We care about students. We are thrilled to have college students in our midst, and we highly value the distinctive, dynamic flavor they bring to the CPC community. We recognize that college students find themselves in one of the most joyful yet challenging, hope-filled yet terrifying, liberating yet stressful stages in their lives. We welcome students at precisely that place, not asking them to be something they’re not, but rather glorying in what they are and helping them grow as people of faith.

 

            We care about the university. We don’t see the university campus is just a convenient pretext for meeting students and talking to them about Jesus. We recognize that the life of the university in all its parts can be extremely beneficial to students, and that their academic work serves as their primary calling while at college. For this reason we don’t disparage the university or its mission; instead, we help students give rightful place to each of the various spheres in which they live, helping live out the gospel in each one.

 

            We care about community. We pray that Christ Pres will be a haven for college students—a place where they are loved and accepted as they are. But we also challenge students not to stay as they are, but to be transformed within the matrix of this congregation and to develop and employ their God-given gifts for the benefit of the entire community at CPC.

 

            We care about the Bible and the mind. At Christ Pres college students are called to interact with the Bible in a dynamic and thoughtful way, applying it to the whole of their lives. We are not content to show students simply how to live in the so-called “sacred” world of church and on-campus fellowship; we go beyond this world to the broader world of work and play and friendship and love and sex and relationships. In short, we care about a biblical world-and-life view, because we think that Christianity is a way of seeing all of life, not just its overtly spiritual components.

 

            We care about the future. Specifically, we care about students’ futures. We know that in most cases we’ll have four years or less to contribute to students’ growth in grace, so we counsel them with an eye to the future. We know that students are at college to be equipped for a lifetime of learning and development, and we seek to equip them in the same vein. If we find that in ten, twenty, or thirty years, former CPC college students are living Christ-infused lives in their workplaces, in their families, and at their churches, we will know that God has answered our fervent prayers.

 

How do we live out our values?

 

            CPC’s college ministry offers several avenues to students by which the above values might be realized:

 

            Sunday Worship 

Students join the broader CPC community to renew our covenant with God in worship, and to be built up and strengthened in Christ through the preaching of the Word, prayer, worship in song, and the weekly celebration of the Lord’s Supper. Sundays at 8:15 and 11:15 a.m.

 

            The College Class

Students gather with others like them to engage God’s word in community. In the college class we think about such questions as: How can I know what is true? How can I be right with God? How can I change? How can I live and share the good news of Jesus? Sundays at 10 a.m.

 

            Reformed University Fellowship

RUF is the incarnation of Christ Pres on the Yale campus (and, in the future, other local campuses as well). At the weekly meeting we sing to God together and interact with the Bible as applied specifically to college students. In our small groups we gather to study the Bible in greater depth and to hold one another accountable to the gospel and its implications. RUF also offers students opportunities to serve their community and to engage the world through mission trips.

 

            CPC Student Partnership

As a way of investing in the larger community at Christ Pres, college students take it upon themselves to love and mentor CPC high school students. We meet together once a month for encouragement and fellowship at various families’ homes. Meanwhile, college students keep up with their high school prayer partners by phone, e-mail, IM, and whatever other way they choose; through these relationships of trust students spur one another one to live out their faith in Christ.

 

            CPC Internships

Christ Pres makes available internships both for current students and for recent alumni. These internships have three goals: theological training, character development, and ministry training. Through the CPC internship students ground themselves more deeply in the faith they profess, enjoy transforming and challenging relationships with ministry leaders in the church, and invest themselves deeply in one of the church’s ministries.
