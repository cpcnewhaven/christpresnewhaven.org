7.0 Security

Fran & Gary W reported from various conversations with the staff & Session. We should take reasonable precautions & have a case-by-case approach to unusual situations.  There are however some general guidelines for people who come asking for money:

Direct them to local ministries that help these people (They are listed on the bulletin board.)

Never give in to their request for money because this almost always leads to more requests & ends in a negative encounter once the handout expectations are not met.

 

Related to the recent problem with one particular man who has been coming around asking for money & eventually made something that sounded like a threat related to our children: We need to make it clear that he is not to come back & if he does call the police.

 

The Session has suggested that the SLB should know of this plan.  We discussed how we might get these thoughts to the entire congregation in an appropriate way.  Email was suggested as better than putting it in bulletin.  Katie will ask the Session.
