FALL CLEANING DIRECTIONS & SCHEDULE

Priority 1 Tasks
Every week we must complete the following tasks:
-Clean bathrooms in the Study Center & new building
-sweep sanctuary and foyer
-dust chairs in sanctuary
-empty all trashcans
(We want our place of worship and other rooms to be clean for Sundays)

Priority 2 Tasks
Each week the team needs to complete as many Priority 2 tasks as you can, given the amount of people and time you have.  Inside the cleaning closet door, there will be a list of other Priority 2 tasks and a place to sign
& date when the job has been completed.  For instance, vacuum and dust
rooms in the Study Center --parlor, lecture room, stairs, foyer,
conference room, copy room, library

Thanks for being diligent and faithful to help take care of our facility.
 You are greatly appreciated!  *Key holders and by default team coordinators. 

Please coordinate with one another what time of day you want to clean.
