General Duties:

Set-up

  • Arrive as soon after 5:30 as you can
  • Clear daycare stuff:  We break down the most of the ‘dividing wall’ near the north end (leaving only the first few segments near the sink), and pile stuff in the northwest and northeast corners.  We clear the hallway that leads back toward the elevator, placing most of this into the daycare director’s office.
  • Unlock doors-at least one glass door and the front (Bradley St.) door, along with the daycare entrance doors.
  • Set-up our stuff .  Put the stand for Tollivar in the center of the north side.  Bring down at least one long table from the lecture room upstairs and set it in the hallway for food.  Bring out about 10 round tables from downstairs closet (~8 chairs each) and put them in the main part of the room.  Drinks are set up on the bar into the kitchen (the two coolers worth, and coffee if possible).  Bring down from the upstairs closet cups, plates, forks, etc. –I’d recommend against tablecloths.  Set out the basket (should be in upstairs closet) to collect meal money.  Help Fran out with whatever she needs for the meal – currently the caterers come at about 6:10.  You may also want to bring down an extra trash can (the one by the upstairs bathrooms)

 

 

Take-down:

  • Remove everything we set up:  This is usually a largely communal activity.
  • Clean up: Dump the extra drinks and coffee.  Take out the trash.  Try to leave the place looking fairly neat.  Again, hopefully many will pitch in for this
  • We do not need to set up the day care stuff.  That will be done by the hope staff. 
  • Adjust the thermostats.  Team leader should know what the wisdom of the day is regarding these.
  • Lock up of find someone to lock up.- again this is the responsibility of the team leader.

Let me know if I’ve forgotten anything or the directions aren’t clear,

~Evan
