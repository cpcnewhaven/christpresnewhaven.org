Dear Friend,

 

 

We want to personally welcome you to Christ Presbyterian Church!  Regardless of your personal and/or spiritual context, we want you to know that you ARE welcome at CPC.  I also hope that you will visit us sometime so that you can meet some of the wonderful, and diverse, people that God has gathered at CPC.   And to be sure, it is our desire to believe in a gospel where grace really is AMAZING, as it is “the power of God unto salvation,” even as it has the power to transform every aspect of our lives.   Regardless of whether or not you are a believer in Christ, or someone who is curious about Christian faith, we want you to feel at home with us!  

 

We also view your visit on our website  as divinely “providential” such that we want to take the opportunity to introduce ourselves to you, if per chance we can also have the opportunity to meet you some day as well. We realize that it is easy for people to feel overwhelmed in any church, and more so to the degree that you are perhaps exposed to things not readily familiar.  I have no doubt that if you visit us, there will be something about CPC that fells strange to you, as every church is different.  But don’t let this mislead you into thinking that we are a people that somehow “have it all together.”  We don’t!  Even as we want CPC to be a place that is “safe” to be morally flawed, even as then to discover the exhilarating grace of the gospel to all who receive and rest upon Christ alone to know God’s favor, and as the basis for how we relate to each other as well.  

 

In order to further introduce you to Christian Faith and CPC, I would recommend that you check out the “Q& A with our pastor” and “Intro to CPC” portions of our website especially.  You will notice that in the “Q& A with the Pastor, ” I have tried to succinctly summarize the kinds of things that I have said many times over the years, in response to the most frequently asked questions by those who visit our church.  Whether you are spiritually “searching,” wondering about “the church thing” or at a place where you are intentionally looking for a church to join—I think you will find this Q& A session helpful.   Otherwise, we have compiled a brief summary of our basic ministries, commitments and history in our Introducing CPC” that we hope will encourage you to visit us so that we can get to know you as well. 

 

I hope to talk with you soon.  And please do not hesitate to contact us if you have any questions or needs regarding a spiritual matter.  

 

I am affectionately yours in Christ,

 

 

Preston Graham Jr.
