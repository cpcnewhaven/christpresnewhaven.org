Biography – Preston D. Graham, Jr.



Preston D. Graham Jr. is the senior pastor at Christ Presbyterian Church (PCA), a thriving church he planted in downtown New Haven reaching the Yale and New Haven communities.  Within five years, his church planted a daughter church in downtown Providence, Rhode Island.

 

Rev. Graham presently serves as the chairman of the Missions Team for the Southern New England Presbytery and as an assessor for the PCA Church Planting Assessment Center.  Rev. Graham is an adjunct lecturer at Gordon-Conwell Theological Seminary teaching ecclesiology.

 

Rev. Graham has published several books, including most recently his Yale thesis titled  A Kingdom NOT of This World: Stuart Robinson's Struggle to Distinguish the Sacred From the Secular During the Civil War (Mercer University Press, 2002).

 

Rev. Graham earned his Masters of Divinity at Gordon Conwell Theological Seminary, his Masters in Sacred Theology in American Religious History at Yale University, and is currently a D. Min. candidate at Pittsburgh Theological Seninary. 

 

Preston and his wife, Lisa,  have been married for 21 years and have three children, Stephen, Nathan, and Anna.
