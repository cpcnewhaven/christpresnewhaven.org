<!--Reformed University Fellowship is the college ministry of the Presbyterian Church in America.  The associate pastor, Rev. Tom Morrison, heads the local chapter, Reformed Christian Fellowship (RCF), at Yale University.  -->RCF seeks to communicate the gospel of Jesus Christ on the college campus, to build Christ's Church, and to equip students to live all of life under Christ.  One of the purposes is to teach the reformed doctrines of grace and apply them to college life and beyond.  This purpose is worked out in the large group meeting at which the Scripture is studied together, in small group Bible studies, and during individual discussions throughout the semester.  RCF also sponsors retreats, conferences, and service projects and spends time together at various socials.  Students from any and all walks of life are welcome, including those who have questions about Christianity.  Students from a wide variety of church and religious backgrounds are involved at Yale.  RCF is for the "convinced" and the "unconvinced."
