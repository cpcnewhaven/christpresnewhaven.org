<h2 align=center style='text-align:center'><a name=cpctop>Fellowship Groups at
CPC </a></h2>

<p class=MsoNormal align=center style='text-align:center'><span
style='mso-bookmark:cpctop'>(For upcoming meeting times, please consult the </span><a
href="javascript:parent.location='../oth/bulletinannouncements.htm'">Bulletin
Announcements</a>.) </p>

<p>Another and very important way that you can get involved in “fellowship” at
CPC is to participate in a fellowship group. A fellowship group is structured
to be “the church in miniature,” a kind of “house church” albeit informally
expressed within closer geographical proximity to where a person lives. The
purpose of the fellowship group is to complement the one <span class=SpellE>anothering</span>
we experience corporately as a whole in a more personal and more readily
available way. Toward this end, there is most likely a fellowship group in your
neighborhood! Each group will participate together in informal teaching,
worship, sharing, prayer and in the provision of mutual help for one another in
times of need. Each group is assigned a trained “facilitator,” mercy
coordinator, host/hostess and worship leader. We strongly encourage everyone in
the church to participate in a “fellowship” group in order to experience the
life of God as expressed more locally and intimately through a small group
context! </p>
