A Study in Confessional Theology

Course Syllabus

(Revised May 10, 2002)

 

Purpose:          

The intention of this class will be to construct a theological system which is consistent with the revelation of God as contained in the Old and New Testament scripture.  However, we will not be doing it alone!  We will utilize that unbroken tradition of interpretation distinguished as the classic evangelical and reformed faith.  The course assumes a confessional approach  rather than a sceptical/apologetic one.  Continuity within the historic reformed tradition will be illustrated from our primary text, The Westminster Confession of Faith, together with readings which seek to span the duration of church history. 

 

Note: For a more foundational study of theology, see “Studies Using the Apostles Creed”:

www.christpresnewhaven.org/sts/biblestudy/foundations.html

 

 

Method:

The method of this class consists of directed readings together with a study guide.[1]  Each Study Guide is linked to the syllabus under its appropriate theological category. Each student is encourage to set apart approximately 4 hours of reading time per topic. Readings can either be purchased from Christ Presbyterian Church, New Haven or are available on reserve in the church study center at 135 Whitney Ave. New Haven.

 

Semester 1:  Theology I consist of lessons 1-11

Semester 2: Theology II consists of lessons 12-22.
