"God, the Father Almighty..."  The Character of God

Introduction: The attributes of God and the limitations of language

Notice that creed places "Father" and  "almighty" together as primary attributes of God.  God is both powerful and personal.  Why is this significant? What if God were only powerful? Only personal?
 

WCF Chapter II. Sect.I.  -  There is but one only living, and true God, who is infinite in being and perfection, a most pure spirit, invisible, without body, parts, or passions; immutable, immense, eternal, incomprehensible, almighty, most wise, most holy, most free, most absolute; working all things according to the counsel of his own immutable and most righteous will, for his own glory; most loving, gracious, merciful, long-suffering, abundant in goodness and truth, forgiving iniquity, transgression, and sin...

II. "Father"
 
    A. Universally
 
        The Scriptures reveal God to be not only the creator but the "father" of all humanity.
 

Luke 3:38 - In the last in a genealogical, Adam is called, "son of God" (literal and federal).
Acts 17:26-28 - He has made one blood all the nations of men to dwell on the face of the earth....
He is not far from every one of us; for in him we live and move and have our being as certain also of your own poets have our being as certain also of your own poets have said, "For we are also his offspring."
Eph. 4:6 - One God and Father of all

Implications:
To believe in the true God is to know him -- a "person" with personal attributes together with relational privileges and responsibilities.  God as father is capable of love, anger, jealousy, mercy, justice, volition, desires, purpose, etc.  The problem of sin, therefore, is not the breach of a moral code, but the break of a relationship.
 

We have been created by a wise, all powerful and all good God who has created us to love and serve Him and to enjoy an eternal relationship with Him.
                                        Rick Lints

III. How, then, do we become children of the covenant? By adoption.
Adoption is that act by which a person takes the child of another into the place, and entitles him/her to the privileges of his own.
 

John 1:12, "but to allwho received him, who believed in his name, he gave power to become children of God, who were botn not of blood or of the will of  the flesh or of the will of man, but of God.
