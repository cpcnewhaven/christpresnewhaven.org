Interpreting the Prophets

A. Historical Context:

Prophets lived in period which called especially for covenant enforcement mediation.

God leaving a "paper trail" of His warnings before judgment

God preparing for the salvation through the Messiah.

Political and Social History inter-weaved with religious history

B. Literary Forms:

Examples: Types of prophetic oracles

1. Covenant Lawsuit (Is. 3:13-26; Hos. 3:3-17, etc.)

1. Court convenes: (13-14)

2. Indictment: (14b-16)

3. Evidence: context

4. Judgment sentence: (17-26)

2. Woe oracle: A cry of "grief" when facing disaster or death like at a funeral.

Example Habakkuk 2:6-8; Micah 2:1-5; Zeph. 2:5-7

1. Announcement of distress

2. Reason fro distress

3. Prediction of doom.

3. Promise Oracle: or "salvation oracle"

Example: Amos 9:11-15

1. Future mentioned "in that day"

2. Restoration described as a radical change

3. Blessing in covenantal categories (see redemptive historical chart)

I.e. Temporal covenant = temporal categories = prefiguring spiritual blessing in Christ
