THEOLOGY II SYLLABUS
(Subject to later revision)
Instructor: Preston Graham, Jr.

Purpose:
The intention of this class will be to construct a theological system which is consistent with the revelation of God as contained in the Old and New Testament scripture. Since all such attempts must assume some point of view, this study will seek to be from within that unbroken tradition of interpretation distinguished as the classic evangelical and reformed faith. The course assumes a confessional approach rather than a sceptical/apologetic one. Continuity within the historic reformed tradition will be illustrated from readings which seek to span the duration of church history.

Method:
The method of this class will consist of directed readings together with class discussion. Clearly then the quality of discussion during our meetings will largely depend upon each participants ability to contribute from his/her weekly readings. Each student is expected to put at least 4 hours of reading time into each class. (Only two hours per week given a bi-weekly class schedule) Readings will either be provided for purchase or made available on reserve in the church study center. Theology I consist of lessions 1-14 and meets every other week for an academic year. Theology II consists of lessons 15-28 and meets every other week for a second academic year. A study guide to each lesson is provided and can be linked from each section of this study guide. (The study guides are complete but will take some time to be formated into this homepage)
