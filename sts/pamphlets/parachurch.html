Re. CPC Statement on Church and Para-Church Relations:

 

A Brief Summary of Statements:

 

#1-- We believe the Scriptures teach and the consensus of the Christian tradition has affirmed that the visible church-- as defined by a Christian organization that adheres to the fundamentals of an apostolic confession of faith, sacramental worship and system of ecclesiastical government--  is an essential element of the gospel whose mission is to make disciples of Jesus Christ.[1]

 

#2— We believe the Scriptures teach and the consensus of the Christian tradition has affirmed that church membership is an essential element of Christian discipleship whereby the means of grace are made effectual unto salvation and Christian growth by the Holy Spirit in God’s perfect wisdom as predicated upon divine election. [2]

 

#3—  We believe the Scriptures teach and the consensus of the Christian tradition has affirmed that there is an authorized ministry of the word, sacrament and pastoral care/government as recognized through ecclesiastical ordination within the visible church in succession to the apostolic foundation whereby Christ mediates his saving presence in the present age as through the “laying on of hands.”[3]

 

#4—We maintain that the kingdom of God, while inclusive of the local church, is not limited to the local church and that God is acting in the world to extend his grace and truth as revealed in Jesus Christ by the power of the Holy Spirit through personal/lay witness, teaching and leadership.[4] 

 

#5—We recognize and affirm the value of Christian organizations that specifically target certain demographically homogeneous groups for the sake of Christian witness, training and leadership specific to the unique needs and lifestyles of that particular group in relation to the expansion of the Kingdom of God. 

 

#6—It has been our long time practice to cooperate with various Christian organizations and value various para-church initiatives especially in so far as it is deemed helpful for the sake of encouraging Christian witness, evangelism and unity in so far as the “cooperative” expresses at least the fundamentals of the Christian faith.[5]

 

#7— While CPC emphasizes the importance and benefits of church membership, such emphasis should not be interpreted to mean that we believe meaningful church membership precludes  participation in evangelical para-church organizations.  To the contrary, we recognize that such involvement can be beneficial as long as it does not hinder authentic membership and participation  in a local church..[6]

 

In Summary:

The people of CPC include university professors, deans, undergraduates, graduates and staff—all of which are people who work in, love and support the university such as to appreciate all efforts to bring God’s saving grace to it no matter what the organizational context!    And yet we are likewise a people who affirm the positive aspects of discipleship in the context of a local church—believing that this is relevant for students and university employees, no less than anyone else in our city. We humbly believe that our counsel concerning the church as an essential element of the gospel supports that which Christ himself established, even that “mediatorial body of Christ” whereby Christ is said to “fill all in all” in the apostolic foundation of ministry that is continued through the visible church.[7]  In the words of Leslie Newbigin

 

The Bible is not (merely) the story of ideas about God, but the story of the people of God…the gospel does not come to each of us in isolation.  It comes to us through a particular book and through a particular fellowship… it is a false spirituality, divorced from the whole teaching of the Bible which regards this visible and continuing church as of subordinate importance for the life of Christ."[8]

 

As a church, we therefore seek to have a proactive and authentic ministry in the midst of our university town, hoping to work with those of like mind across evangelical denominations and other evangelical organizations in the things of the gospel for the glory of Christ.
