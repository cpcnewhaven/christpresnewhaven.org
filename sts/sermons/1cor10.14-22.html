Bannerman:

The koinonia--the communion, (or participation, or interchange, or mutual fellowship) of the blood of Christ and the body of Christ--- cannot possibly be understood as the mere signs of the body and blood, without a very violent experiment practiced on the language of the apostle. And if "fellowship" does not refer to the outward symbol, it can only refer to the spiritual blessings represented in the ordinance-- to Christ Himself present after a spiritual manner in the Sacrament, and giving himself to the believer, while the believer gives himself to Christ, so as to establish a true "koinonia", or fellowship, or communion between them.
