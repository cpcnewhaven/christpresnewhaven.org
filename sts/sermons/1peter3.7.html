An Understanding Husband

Introduction:

It's your turn husbands!

Exposition:

Likewise (related to wives and husbands..) Husbands live according to understanding (knowledge) as with a weaker (lesser in authority) female vessel, showing honor as even co-heirs together of the grace of life in order that your prayers may not be hindered.

Likewise-- - or "next" as in a transition...

Although the apostle is elaborate in his discussion on the duties of the wife and relatively brief in his instruction to the husband, the significance of his exhortations to the husband ought not to be taken lightly... for rather than extensively dealing with the husbands role in general (as say in Eph.5), Peter is specifically addressing the husbands role in relation to the things he has just taught concerning the wives..

Important-- that we see these instructions as related to to the previous instructions- thus by "likewise"- he is particularly concerned to show a husbands duty with respect to the fact that the wife is of "lesser authority" in the husband/wife relationship so as not then to abuse the relationship...

Translation note-- 3 participles...

I. What the Husband Understands

A. The peculiar social position that a wife must comply with...

live according to understanding (knowledge) as with a weaker (lesser in authority) female vessel,

weaker vessel = lesser in authority-- previous exhortation concerning the wifes reponsibility to subordinate herself to the husband, not because he is superior intellectually, physically, spirituality or morally, but because of the institution of God in marriage as an expression of his government in this world... she is to subordinate herself to the office, less the person himself, as an office instituted by God... thus, hers is a position of social "weakness" or "lesser" authority...

The husband is to live with knowledge concerning this...

yet especially given who is wife is!

B. Why Peculiar-- because of the second thing a husband understands...

as even co-heirs together of the grace of life

sugklhronomo--inheriting together with-- here 'fellow heirs with christ... from verb "to close up together, hem in, enclose-- i.e. to be put in the same category... to be destined for the same situation!

Rom. 8:17 and if children, then heirs--heirs of God and joint heirs with Christ, if indeed we suffer with Him, that we may also be glorified together.

Eph. 3:6 that the Gentiles should be fellow heirs, of the same body, and partakers of His promise in Christ through the gospel,

"Of the grace of life"-- clearly talking about her privileged status "along side of him" in heaven...

Rom. 5:21 so that as sin reigned in death, even so grace might reign through righteousness to eternal life through Jesus Christ our Lord.

Titus 3:7 that having been justified by His grace we should become heirs according to the hope of eternal life.

Thus-- co-heirs...grace of life-- wife is every bit the equal to a husband in eternal status-- the husband then must live understanding that his authority is only provisional and so as not to negate her equality for all eternity...

In heaven, a place where marital authority will be abolilshed (see Christ) so as then to be equal, not just in intellect, morality, spirituality and our new spiritual bodies-- but also in authority, as everyone equally under the immediate authority of Christ.

while husband enjoy authority in the present age as over his wife, it is temporary, and we ought to live with an eye to this reality--

DON'T CONFUSE A TEMPORARY, ALBEIT PROVISIONAL ARANGEMENT WITH THE ETERNAL AND HEAVENLY RELALITY

I.E. IN CHRIST, THERE IS NEITHER MALE NOR FEMALE-- (GAL.3:28)

Illustration-- equal honor, worth-- a king living under the authority of another king under a different jurisdiction. Someone who is every way "honorable" yet within a jurisdictioin that as him subordinate to the authorities that be...

Why the need to exhort "respect/honor"-- because she is "weaker" in authority so as to not be confused with weaker in honor!

Note-- in our modern world-- often it is assumed that more authority = more honor-- this is NOT the case necessarily--

Note: AS this works out in the wife who subordinates herself to her husband-- she finds complete fulfillment in her husband in so far as she is honored by his authority.

I.e. Consider that God has given the man the authority whereby his honoring of his wife is all the more significant, since it is honor bestowed by one in whom God has granted authority.

I.e. its one thing to be honored by say a private if you are a captain-- its another thing to be honored by a general if you are a captain-- the women then is "honored" by one greater in authority, yet as one equal in honor...

Results: that your prayers may not be hindered...

What exactly does this mean?

1. Perhaps it is that to deny her honor as a co-heir would practically and literally get in the way of their praying together.

I.e. prayer together assumes equal access to God such that each person, as under a common priest (Christ) and as each having the same access to God- can each petition God so as to have equal time so to speak.. To deny honor to the wife would be to in effect deny this about her so as to prevent her prayers, in the mind of hte husband, as being as efficacious as his own... thus the effect to "hinder" your prayers... as together.

2. Perhaps speaking from experience (1 Cor.9:5), Peter understands that when a husband fails to live with his wife in an understanding way, and the wife refuses to accept the authority of her husband, there is an inability to pray with each other...

3. Perhaps there is an even more profound point to this phrase however-- in so far as prayer is often associated with calling-- we pray in relation to our calling...

Peter is here then suggesting that a man's calling, as indicated even by his prayers, would be hindered if he failed to honor his wife.

I.e. He would be a failure or at least "hindered" in his duties as a husband because he failed to honor his wife.. stated differently, his prayers as concerning his vocation as husband would be denied since his prayers, premised on the false assumption of his own superiority in person, would be denied...

I.e. the husbands prayers regarding his calling in relation to his wife might be hindered by virtue of their false requests...

...prayers that are out of the will of God are themselves hindered...

So for example-- if I pray to get rich, these prayers may in fact be hindered since they are outside of the will of God is so far at least that they are selfcentered and selfish...

so then, here "hindered" here means "answered in the negative", etc.

So that whatever else we pray for our wives as those called to pray for them-- our prayers will be hindered to the degree that they don't recognize her worth to God and her equal value as joint heirs to eternal life...

 

Mainpoint: The Understanding Husband takes into consideration that his wife's lesser authority is only provisional under the present age and that she is every bit the equal in honor as co-heirs together in Christ so that he extends his temporal authority so as to bestow honor upon his wife that is rightfully hers for eternity.
