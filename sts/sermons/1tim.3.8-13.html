IV. Blessed Promise!

(13) for those who serve well gain for themselves a good reputation and also great assurance in the faith which (is) according to Jesus Christ.

2 promises:

1. They will be honored: Paul permits the church to "honor" godly people as such.. their "good reputation" should be assumed once they have proven themselves,...

2. The Gospel will be honored:

The work of deacon, accomplished by a Godly person, will do honor to the gospel,

Mainpoint: The Deacon's Resume consist of his being a man of principle whose Christian character is evidenced in his personal life and social relations.

Discussion: 2 reflections...

1. Need to rethink the fundamental unit of society.

Does the church consist of individuals, or is the fundamental unit social groupings-- NT socieity consist of a congregate of individuals related to each other by a social contract...

Therfore, Godliness is measured not by "personal success" or merely Invidual qualities, but by social success, how this person's faith impacts his social relations...

2.People of conviction vs. people of preferences!

There are many things which any person prefers, this passage elludes to them

1. Popularity

2. Having fun

3. Worldly gain (wealth)

And sadly, a persons achievement of these "preferences" are often what is confused with leadership...

I.e. resume: Are the successful = popular, fun and wealthy...

In modern America, leadership is often confused with popularity-- a leader (politically) is know less for his ideas and more for his ability not to offend.

In modern America, leadership is often confused with individual ambition-- a leader is therefore someone who has accomplished his/her goals--

In modern America, leadership is often confused with secular success-- he has risen up the ranks of his profession...

Biblically, a leader is someone whose ambition is for God and whose leadership is out of conviction for the profit of the collective whole vs. only him/her self.

-This person has proven himself capable of loving, even when it doesn't suit his individual preferences

-This person has proven himself capable of putting the interests of the collective whole before his own!

-This person has proven himself capable of distinguishing his preferences from his convictions and acting accordingly.
