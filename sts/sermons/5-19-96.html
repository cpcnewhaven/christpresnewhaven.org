Text: Mat. 27.45-54
The Accomplishment of the Cross-- The Narrative of Christ's Death

Introduction: Obituaries record what a person accomplished with his life, not what the person accomplished with his death. Here, by narrating the circumstances of Christ's death, Matthew details what Christ accomplished through his death. This passage, like so much of Matthew's gospel, demonstrates that in the "story" Matthew has written a theology.

Exposition:
Matthew frames the death of Christ in such manner so as to signify what is being accomplished. Steeped in symbols, which we would not want to miss...
For instance,notice how...

I.The circumstances of "Darkness" signifies what is being accomplished by the Death of Christ

45) From the sixth hour (noon) darkness come upon the entire land until the ninth hour (3 pm)

OT predicts such darkening to be a sign of God's judgment.

Amos 8:8 "Because of this will not the land quake And everyone who dwells in it mourn? Indeed, all of it will rise up like the Nile, And it will be tossed about, And subside like the Nile of Egypt. 9 "And it will come about in that day," declares the Lord God, "That I shall make the sun go down at noon And make the earth dark in broad daylight.
Joel 2:30 I will show portents in the heavens and on the earth, blood and fire and columns of smoke. 31 The sun shall be turned to darkness, and the moon to blood, before the great and terrible day of the LORD comes.

IN prophetic announcements of judgment, darkness often appears as a sign of God's wrath and of impending ruin (Amos 8:9) and particularly as indicative of the "Day of the Lord" Joel 2:31-- the day when God would visit the earth with judgment so as to be victorious over evil Therefore, the darkening was a sign of God's wrath agains the earth, and anger so great that He turned his back on him utterly and withheld for a time even common grace contained in the light of the sun. This indicates that the judgment in the end time has already begun.

II. The passion of Jesus signifies what is being accomplished at Christ's death.

46) And around the ninth hour Jesus raised his voice with a great (mega) sound saying "Eli, Eli, lema sabachthani" which means "My God, My God, why have you deserted me?"
A. Jesus loudly testifies that God has forsaken him with the words:

46: " My God, My God, why hast Thou forsaken Me?".

A quote from Psa. 22. Not really a question to be answered; rather, an expression of grief and torment, but also a testimony to the reality that he suffered!

H. Ridderbos:
For Jesus to be handed over to the enemies was nothing less than to be abandoned to the power of darkness and the power of the evil one.

Christ was enduring the wrath of God, God's favor had forsaken him! Forsaken but not unfaithful. But notice also how Christ remained faithful... his utterance of the prophetic lament from Psalm 22 shows that no matter how desperate he had become, he remained within the limits of obedience and neither rebelled against God nor gave up hope in God's plan for Him. Notice also "MY God, MY God"
B. The Voluntary resignation of his life

50) But after Jesus cried out again with a loud voice he resigned(let go of) the spirit.

Note the active description, "yielded up His spirit," (as opposed to "he died.")
John 19:30-- "It is finished" He gave it up voluntarily and consciously.
C. The Reaction from those near him to Christ's passion:

47)When some of the ones standing there heard (it) they said that he is calling for Elijah." 48) and imediately one ran from them and got a sponge, filled it with sour wine (vinegar) and wrapped it around a reed and gave it to him. 49) But other said, "wait, let us see if Elijah comes to save him. .

1. Vinegar and water: Was considered better than water in quinching thirst-- used by soildiers and lower class people as a substitute for wine.

2. Mistakened call for Elijah:

a. According to Jewish myth, Elijah was like an angel because he had been taken into heaven alive, and he therefore cold come back to help the godly at a moments notice.

2Kings 2:11 As they continued walking and talking, a chariot of fire and horses of fire separated the two of them, and Elijah ascended in a whirlwind into heaven.
b. Elijah was expected to come "before"the great and terrible day of the Lord (John the Baptist)

Mal. 4:5 Lo, I will send you the prophet Elijah before the great and terrible day of the LORD comes.

Could be that they rightly understood Elijah as the forerunner to God's great and terrible day of judgment, such that they though Christ was crying out for Judgment. Would indicate that they still did not believe that Chrit was the Messiah

Regardless, the soildiers recognized with Christ that his death was somehow related to judgement.


IIII. The Consequences of Christ's death signify what is being accomplished by the Death of Christ, 51-54.

A. Note the "And behold", 51. Matthew's way of making sure we see the significance.

B. Three consequenses which tell of Christ's accomplishment by his death

1. Veil of temple torn from top to bottom, Old Covenant Significance: Represented the barrier of God's judgment so as to prevent free access to God's blessed and lifegiving presence

Gen. 3:24 He drove out the man; and at the east of the garden of Eden he placed the cherubim, and a sword flaming and turning to guard the way to the tree of life.
Ex. 26:33 You shall hang the curtain under the clasps, and bring the ark of the covenant in there, within the curtain; and the curtain shall separate for you the holy place from the most holy.

And on this curtain was embroidered a picture of cherubim and sword, indicating the covenant curse of Gen.3:24. Inside, was the mercy seat representing God's mercy and blessing whereby Israel could "meet" again with God in his dwelling place. Yet this "meeting" was only after judgment was satisfied upon a substitute sacrifice so that the Priest was instructed to do several things.

Lev. 16:2 The LORD said to Moses: Tell your brother Aaron not to come just at any time into the sanctuary inside the curtain before the mercy seat that is upon the ark, or he will die; for I appear in the cloud upon the mercy seat.
Lev. 4:6 The priest shall dip his finger in the blood and sprinkle some of the blood seven times before the LORD in front of the curtain of the sanctuary.


Therefore, the splitting of the curtain in the temple:

a. indicates that God's wrath against Christ had annuled the function of the curtain in the Temple.

b. the entrance to the sanctuary was symbolically opened to everyone and access to God and His dwelling place was not longer a special privilege of the priests (thus the priesthood of all believers)

Heb. 6:19 This hope we have as an anchor of the soul, a hope both sure and steadfast and one which enters within the curtain, 20 where Jesus has entered as a forerunner for us, having become a high priest forever.
Heb. 10:20 by the new and living way that he opened for us through the curtain (that is, through his flesh),


Broadus:
Symbolized the complete opening for all of a way of access through Christ, our high priest, has entered the true Holy of Holies in heaven, offering once for all the all sufficient atoning sacrifice of his own blood (Heb.9:11-28); and now in his name we may look without dread upon the very throne of god, and come with boldness to the throne of grace. (Heb.4:16; 10:19)


2. Shaking of this creation, and the earth shook; and the rocks were split. Again, notice the prophetic significance of this observation by Matthew.

Amos 8:8 "Because of this will not the land quake And everyone who dwells in it mourn?
Hag. 2:6 "For thus says the Lord of hosts, 'Once more in a little while, I am going to shake the heavens and the earth, the sea also and the dry land.


A new creation order was introduced by Christ's death by the power of God-- power that will be vindicated at Christ's resurrection. This "new creation" is the execution of God's judgment and delieverance so as to establish a new kingdom order...

3. Resurrection of the dead,

52) And the tombs were opened and many bodies of the holy ones (saints) who were dead were raised. 53) and when they came out of their tombs after He was raised they entered into the holy city and where made to appear to many.

A temporary return from the dead of many rightous. This is reminiscent of Ezekiel's prophecy concern the day of the Lord--here only as a fortaste of things to come because of the death of Christ.

Ezek. 37:12 "Therefore prophesy, and say to them, 'Thus says the Lord God," Behold, I will open your graves and cause you to come up out of your graves, My people; and I will bring you into the land of Israel. 13 "Then you will know that I am the Lord, when I have opened your graves and caused you to come up out of your graves, My people.


John Calvin:
This was a particular portent in which God testified that His Son had enetered death's prison, not to stay there shut up, but to lead all free who were there held captive. At the time which Christ's person was seen as contemptible weakness of the flesh, the splendid and divine power of his death had reached down even to the underworld"
1Cor. 15:20 ¶ But in fact Christ has been raised from the dead, the first fruits of those who have died.
All this leads us to ask: What is our response to the death of christ...

V. The centurion's confession, is what Matthew would have all his readers confess in response to what was accompished by the Death of Christ, vs.55- this is the climax for Matthew!

55: "Truly this was the Son of God!".

A royal designation:

2 Sam 7, God speaks to David, 12 "When your days are complete and you lie down with your fathers, I will raise up your descendant after you, who will come forth from you, and I will establish his kingdom. 13 "He shall build a house for My name, and I will establish the throne of his kingdom forever. 14 "I will be a father to him and he will be a son to Me; when he commits iniquity, I will correct him with the rod of men and the strokes of the sons of men,.
Psa. 2:7,"I will surely tell of the decree of the \Lord\: He said to Me, 'Thou art My Son, Today I have begotten Thee.


Jesus, as King, has defeated sin and death, and properly deserves this confession of coronation!

Mainpoint: The Great and terrible day of the Lord has come upon Jesus, upon whom his long expected wrath has been poored out! Jesus is the royal king, the visitation of God upon the earth with judgment and salvation, so that whoever believes in him shall be delievered from sin and escorted into the Kingdom of God's beloved Son!

Christ's deatyh indicate the coming of a new order where heaven and earth are changed into a new heaven and a new earth
This new order brings an end to the temple
This new order brings an end to death and judgment for all who are participants.
This new order even brings in a new haven and earth (darkness and earthquake)
THIS NEW ORDER IS THE KINGDOM OF GOD WHERE THERE IS FORGIVENESS OF SIN AND LIFE EVERLASTING

It began with Christ's death, it will be consumated at Christ's return...
In Christ's death, everything changes!
Old Covenant is now finished
New Kingdom has begun!


Application:
1. We learn what sin deserves: Abandonment by God, and his scorching wrath.
Don't allow God's common grace to confuse you with this reality!
Although you enjoy a life of liesure and pleasure today, the terrible day which came upon Christ is yet to come upon those who do not receive him.

(those who have received Christ-- in effect the terrible day has ALREADY COME AND GONE-- THERE IS NO LONGER SUCH A DAY WAITING FOR YOU!..I.E. If your are NOT on that cross with Christ, then you will face the wrath of god alone. Those who properly partake of this meal (pointing to the Lord's Table) having put their faith in Christ and are admitted by Christ's mediated authority in the church (the divine keys) then you are in effect upon the cross with Christ so that your judgment is over... )

But if you are not prepared at this time to properly partake of this meal, then in effect it symbolizes that you are not on the cross with Christ such that your cross of judgment still awaits you..

Indeed, we learn what sin deserves, and since all of us have sinned, we all deserve what it deserves...

2. We descover the nature of of God's wrath:
a. Abandonment by God...
God is everything good... everything living, everything true, everything pleasurable...
What you enjoy in this world that is good, living, true and pleasurable is the activity of God! It is his marvelous grace the for a time gives it to the wicked and good alike!
Have you enjoyed the birth of a child, it was the activity of God that you enjoyed
Have you enjoyed good music, it wasthe activity of God that you enjoyed...
Have you enjoyed a good meal, it was the activity of God that you enjoyed
Have you enjoyed good art, ..
Have you enjoyed good friends,...
In the presence of God, we enjoy all these things...
In the absence of God, we have none of these things!

Illustration:
Nothing causes us more pain than to hear of children who have tragically lost their parents! Why?
Therefore, in the absence of God, there is the absence of our eternal parent, everything good, living, true, pleasurable, etc.

Therefore,
1. Apart from Christ, you are await full abandonment by God!
2. With faith in Christ, you have already been abandoned in Christ, and now restored in Christ as "sons and daughters of God!
Think about this! IF you have felt abandoned by God, Christ was abandoned by God so that you would never need to be!

Rom. 8, What can seperate you from the love of God...

The point of Rom. 8 is to assure you that no matter what happens, you are not abandoned.. even what may look like abandonment is not!

3. We learn that Christ is enthroned as king of a new creation. He inaugurates a new order of things!
Christ's sacrifice opens the way to God, by a new and living way-- sweeping away Levitical sacrifices, annuling the burden of the law, and ushering in a new life and the future resurection of our bodies to be with the Lord forever...

3. We learn what Matthew believes would be our proper response--
We ought to join with the centurion in confessing Christ as the king,
We ought to confess him as God's son and receive from him access into God's holy presence where there isthe forgiveness of sin

Think about this:
How many times have utopian visions been desired and codified into novels, movies, etc.
We hear about a new deal, a new society, the new education, etc... all with hopes of ushering in a new kingom of sorts.. But only Christ did this, and can do this for you!

Such is the accomplishment of Christ byHis death, for all who would receive him! Indeed, Obituaries record what a person accomplished with his life, today God has informed you about what Jesus accomplished with his death.
Are you prepared to participate in Christ's dying by faith, that you might participate in his living by faith-- then you are prepard to enjoy the blessing of this table here before you! etc. etc.
