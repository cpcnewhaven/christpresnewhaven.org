Introduction

We all know what a rival is-- perhaps you learned the word first in the context of sport-- that "rival" team was the team that competed against your team for victory! In watching a game then-- it is almost second nature to decide which team you will pull for…

(Who are you pulling for Dad?)

Well, in life we are also faced with rivals-- far from being a "sport" it’s the real thing-- competing powers, competing loyalties-- all asking for our trust and loyalty-- all promising fulfillment and "life."

What are some of those rivalries-- especially as pertains to what promises to make us fulfilled and complete as humans?

We will return to this later--but in the progress of redemption, we find ourselves in Gen. 14-- and an ancient rivalry is presented--

At first-- the rivalry seems to be between competing political/military powers… of which Abram is sucked in…

Chapter 14 is in essence describes in great detail a typical "International" skermish in the ancient world in which powerful city-states form coalitions and battle it out for territory. We have here the first account of "war" in the Bible-- We know that such wars were common for ancient Mespotamian kings.

But as the story winds up-- we discover that the true rivalry that Abram faces is that which would require his loyalty to either political/military coalitions on the one hand or God on the other… How will Abram choose, and what will the story tell us about life itself?

Let's see.
