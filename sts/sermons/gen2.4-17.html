II. A Covenant God

Notice the introduction here of "Yaweh" to "elohim" (6 times)

Everytime you see "Lord" in front of "God" it is the word "Yahewh" or Yehweh God...

Creation proper (Gen.1:1-2:3) only the generic term for God, Elohim, is used for the Creator. In the narrative of human history in the garden (2:4-3:24-- which sets into motion the redemptive history proper), Yahweh-Elohim is used.. This compound divine name is used as often within this brief compass as it is in all the rest of the OT. .. clearly the combination of the name Y with E in the pre-fall history of Gen. 2 and 3 is designed as a pointed declaration that Y, the lord of redemptive history, is E, the Creator-God.

Ie--the covenant name of God therefore a covenant history begins...

B. The Sacramental Trees

Covenant symbols-- as sacraments...

I.e. a "sacramental presence..."

Note the 4 rules of a sacrament- each apply here.

1. "The first mark or characteristic of a Sacrament is that it be a Divine institute appointed by Christ for His Church." James Bannerman

2. The next mark of a sacramental ordinance is "that they be sensible and outward signs of spiritual truths; and this mark applies to the ordinance of the Lord's Supper."

3. The third mark of a sacramental ordinance is "that they are the seals of a federal (representative) transaction between the believer and Christ through means of the ordinance; and this mark is applicable to the Lord's Supper."

tree of Life (promise) and Tree of knowledge of Good and evil... (stipulations)

4. "The fourth and last mark "is, that it is a means (instrument) of grace; and this mark also applies to the ordinance of the Lord's Supper." (Bannerman, p.140)
