Nakedness after the fall-- vulnerabity, shame, discontentedness, lose of intimacy and sense of alienation... (from God, from one another, from ourselves, from creation itself)

2. Eve--

To the woman he said, I will doubly multiply (multiply ,multiply) the pain in conception, in pain you shall bring forth children, And to the man you shall desire him and he shall govern you.

As for the curse upon women in childbirth-- there is no real need for explanation... yet it is significant to notice that while God has promised a restored posterity through her womb, it will all the same come through suffering...

What exactly is the curse here in relation to the wife's relation to her husband?

Clearly, Moses does not consider the wives subordination to her husband to be a judgment to sin.

-- woman was made from man to be a helper

-- woman is twice "named" by man (2:23, 3:20) both before and after the fall which in the Hebrew iindicates authority over her.

-- Paul also interprets husbands authority over wives as related to the order of creation itself before the fall.

1. It is often argued then that "rule" here repreetns harsh exploitive subjugation which so often has characterized male/female restionships in societies. i.e. to love and to cherish becomes to desire and to dominate...

Yet, not ignoring that "rule" in all spheres of life including the family has been perverted by sin, this doesn't make the best sense of the passage, especially the idea of woman's "desiring" her husband...

2. Susan Foh (WTJ, 37, 376ff) -- word "desire, urge" referes often (if not exclusively in the Bible) to the urge to master or dominate over something! (a ruling urge)

I.e. Gen.4:7-- it is sin's urge to dominate Cain

sin lies at the door and its "urge" is for (over) you, but you should rule over it...

So here-- the point is that as one aspect of the curse, the woman being "turned over to sin" would result in her desire to be independant and even to dominate her husband-- this in constrast to an injunction to man to govern her...

I.e. Rather than a husbands inclination to govern his wife being viewed as a curse after the fall, it is that the wife would have inclinations to govern her husband, this as her curse in so far as happiness and prosperity will be accomplished when God's kingdom order in the home is honored by both man and women... thus setting up conflict which would need to be restored...

IV. Expressions of Faith -- vs. 20

NAMING HIS WIFE

Significant that it's juxtaposed with the inevitability of death in

v. 19.

Having just heard the pronouncement of death-- Adam names his wife "mother of life"

named her Eve-- the sound play on "hay" (living) and "hawwa" reflects Adams faith that through the spiritual seed of Eve would come life again.. (I.e. Adams response to God's promise in vs. 15-- thus evidence that Adam is one of the elect... )

IV. Divine Clothing/Restoration

And so notice the end, that whatever selse happens here, it will result in new clothing, even as this will be accomplished as through judgment!

(vs. 21) And the Yahweh God made garments (tunics) of skins for the man and for his wife, and clothed them

They are clothed not with plants (and they sewed fig leaves together and made themselves aprons, v.7) but with animal skins, fore-shadowing the shedding of blood, the taking of life necessary to save Eve and Adam.

Covering through death of sacrificial animal.

Evidently, God does not think much of the fig leaves; they don't adequately cover the shameful nakedness. Thus God in his grace takes matters into his own hand to provide a covering.

Result:

A re-establishment of covenant relationship, both between the man and his wife and between them and God.
