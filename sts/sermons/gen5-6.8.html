A. Last week-- the ending of the first"these are the generations of"

1. First-- these are the generations of heaven and earth--(2:4) tracing the general history of creation as through the fall and the evil "generation" of Adam through Cain up to Noah...

Weaved throughout this account of creation is a picture of eschalating sin...

Sin== assuming the perogative of God to determine right from wrong... failing to bring all things under submission to the Creator King-- Yahweh God!

Last week this account of the generations of heaven and earth climaxed with Lamech, who even wrote a song in celebration of his murderous sins...

--God, promising a "seed" who would would deliver a lineage from Adam and Eve from the curse of sin...

Abel's offering of a sacrificied animal, after the pattern instituted by God when he clothed Adam and eve with the skins of a sacrificed animal-- evidenced that Abel was of the lineage promised in the seed of the women through whom would arise a deliverer...

Cain's offering after his own choosing demonstrated he was not of the promised lineage...

Observations:

1. each biographical section ends, recording "natural death" as God's common curse on all.. Clearly the curse of death upon all humanity is being revealed as simultaneous to the continuation of the imago dei...

2. What to make of the ages (according to the MT, which is generally shorter than LXX)

Adam- 930, Seth-- 912, Enosh-- 905, Qenan--910, Mahalalel--895, Yared--962, Enoch--(ascention into heaven) 365, Methuselah--969, Lamek--777

Various options-- no certainty

a- literal-- i.e. "whatever the physiological explanation, a providential purpose was the preservation of humanity still few in number, young in technology, and obliged to cope with the fractious environment.

b-- symbolic

-- 1) numbers working off the sexagesimal system-- where the ages tend to be exact multiples of 5 or multiples of 5 + 7.

--2) numbers related to varoius astronomical periods as related to the time it takes for a planet to return to the same point in the sky-- these astronomical periods were known to the Babylonians and a sexagestiimal arithmetic would have made the calculatoin relatively simple.

Note then: Enoch lived exactly 365 years which would symbolize the perfect span of life...

If symbolic, Why?

-- maybe to reflect merely the orderliness of life before the lood and to convey the passage of time in God's perfect providence-- I.e. that pre-diluvian history was acting under the perfect plan of God...

-- maybe to respond to ancient myths (an anti-Mesopotamian plemic being weaved into the account)-- such that when contrasted with the enormous reign of Kings in Sumerian myth stories (often more than 1,000 years)-- this story then in effect scales down the alleged ages of man's earliest forebears in comparison... such that while the numbers symbolize a "great history" leading up to the fall, these men were in fact "mortal".
