John 13:31-35

United to one another in love

Introduction:

What is the distinguishing mark of the Christian?

What distinguishes the Christian community from all other communities...

Exposition:

So as to comprehend the meaning of vs.34-35 with repect to the command to love

I. Context:

A. v31, Therefore

Textual clue that vs.1-30 is important toward an understanding of love!

vs 1-20, Dramatic demonstration of Christ serving his disciples

vs. 14, If I then, your Lord and TEacher, have washed your feet, you also ought to wash one another's feet. For I have given you an example, that you also should do as I have done to you...

vs. 21-30, The example of Christ's suffering includes sacrifice-- in that Christ's exemplary service would ultimate cost him his very life throught betrayal.

Unmistakenly, the command to "love" is put after Christ's dramatic display of sacrificial SERVICE so as to further clarify the meaning of love.

1. The relation of "love" to "service" is further demonstrated when the word "to love" is used synonomously with the word "to serve" in scripture

Deut. 10:12 ¶ So now, O Israel, what does the LORD your God require of you? Only to fear the LORD your God, to walk in all his ways, to love him, to serve the LORD your God with all your heart and with all your soul,

Josh. 22:5 Take good care to observe the commandment and instruction that Moses the servant of the LORD commanded you, to love the LORD your God, to walk in all his ways, to keep his commandments, and to hold fast to him, and to serve him with all your heart and with all your soul."

Matt. 6:24 ¶ "No one can serve two masters; for a slave will either hate the one and love the other, or be devoted to the one and despise the other. You cannot serve God and wealth.

2. Other instances where love is related to real and practical service in scripture.

1John 3:17 How does God's love abide in anyone who has the world's goods and sees a brother or sister in need and yet refuses help?

2Cor. 8:3 For, as I can testify, they voluntarily gave according to their means, and even beyond their means, 4 begging us earnestly for the privilege of sharing in this ministry to the saints... 8 I do not say this as a command, but I am testing the genuineness of your love against the earnestness of others.

This is to say that just as faith is shown by it's works, so too love is shown by its service!

To "serve" someone verbally is to Love someone in your speech

To "serve" someone materially is to love someone in your giving tangible things.

To "serve" someone spiritually is to love someone by providing them with those things pertaining to their relation to God (prayer, teaching, setting up a worship facility, etc)

A person who loves is a person who serves!

Sadly, in today's sentimentality, we have confused love with a saucy personality.

Having a pleasant or "nice" demenour is not itself love...

Biblically, love is service-- real, time consuming and often times tangible service!

This at least is demonstrated by Christ's dramatic demonstration set securely in the context of this command to love!

B. Love Glorifies God!

This is shown as the command to love is carefully put within the context of vs.31-33 concerning Christ's bringing glory to God.

I.e. The Relation of God's glory as manifest in Christ and "love" through his service to disciples...

John 15:8 My Father is glorified by this, that you bear much fruit and become my disciples. 9 As the Father has loved me, so I have loved you; abide in my love. 10 If you keep my commandments, you will abide in my love, just as I have kept my Father's commandments and abide in his love. 11 I have said these things to you so that my joy may be in you, and that your joy may be complete. John 15:12 "This is my commandment, that you love one another as I have loved you.

1. Not litererally a "new" commandment

2. New as in its being revived in its use and importance. Something unused is "new" although it could have been around for a long time.

a. Note for instance that there are 2 words for "new" in Greek

Kainon vs. neos

neos

1. New, vs. aged old (wine)

2. young

3. novice

Here, in the sense of what a thing actually is?

kainon

1. "unused" (as in wineskins)

2. as in previously not present (unknown, strange, remarakable) (Mk.1:27, Ac17:19, Rv.2:17

3. In contrast to something old or absolete (Eph.2:15, 1 Cor.11:25, 2 Cor.3:6)

Here, in the sense of what manner people relate to it?

a.Examples:

Matt. 9:17 Neither is new wine put into old wineskins; otherwise, the skins burst, and the wine is spilled, and the skins are destroyed; but new wine is put into fresh (unused) wineskins, and so both are preserved."

III. Strategic Promise

(35) In this all will know that you are MY disciples, when you have love by (en, in, for) one another.

I.e. In Christ's day, I would argue is also true for today, that genuine love (service) is so rare, that when we really begin to love each other through service-- it will distinguish the people of God from all other people

So important is this command to love (which is to serve), that Christ here gives the world permission to judge us by whether or not we keep it or not!

Think about it--

Just as true religion is faith that works

So too, Christ is saying that true religion is love that serves...

Mainpoint: The distinguishing mark of true Christianity is that God is glorified in our being united to one another in love-- love that evidences itself in genuine service!

So important is "Love" to true spiritualitiy that our confession makes it the genuine mark of the communion of saints.

WCF 26

1. All saints, that are united to Jesus Christ their head, by his Spirit,and by faith, have fellowship with him in his graces, sufferings, death,resurrection, and glory: and, being united to one another in love, they have communion in each other's gifts and graces, and are obliged to the performance of such duties, public and private, as do conduce to their mutual good, both in the inward and outward man.

Notice then how the confession relates our "Being united to one another in love" to service...

2. Saints by profession are bound to maintain an holy fellowship and communion in the worship of God, and in performing such other spiritual services as tend to their mutual edification; as also in relieving each other in outward things, according to their several abilities and necessities.Which communion, as God offereth opportunity, is to be extended unto all those who, in every place, call upon the name of the Lord Jesus.

Discussion:

Why this passage in preparation for voting on deacons!

Answer:

1. To make an argument for why we need deacons, and why perhaps more ought to consider the calling!

Note: However we understand the "office" of deacons

a. Baptist traditions hold to the office including the duty to rule, (elders are also "deacons")

b. Presbyterian and Independents(Congregational) hold to the office as distinct from the office of those who rule (elder)

All agree that God has distinguished an office so as to insure that churches be genuinely united together in love.

I.e the office of "deacon" is the organizational side to the duty to "love" the outward person just as the office of "elder" is the organizational side to the duty to "love" the inward person.

1. Tim.3:8, Why those who hold this office are called "diakonos" in scripture... "servants" so as to place organization to the command that we be united together in love! "real love, serving love"

2. Why this "service-love" was a distinguishing mark of the early New Testament church.

Acts 22:44, And all who believed were together and had all things in common and they sold their possessions and goods and distributed them to all, as any had need.

Yet in Acts.6:1, we are told that some "widows" were being "neglected in the daily distribution"

So important was this concern to the apostles that in

Acts 6:2 we are told that a select group of people were ordained so as to insure that the church was "united together in love" through its service to one another in tangible assets... even as the apostles continued in their loving service of the word of god and prayer...

Notice carefully this passage:

And the twelve called together the whole community of the disciples and said, "It is not right that we should neglect the word of God in order to serve tables. Therefore brethren, pick out from among you seven men of good repute, full of the Spirit and of wisdom whom we may appoint to this duty.

Point: The distribution of the church's tangible assests (money, worldly stuff, time, helps etc), expecially here the distribution of food, was the tangible evidence of being united together in love.
So importantn was it to maintain this service, that a select group of people were ordained (notice later the laying on of hands) so as to insure that this was accomplished!

Just as the early church took Christ commandment to love one another seriously, so ought we!

Final apologetic in that in the absense of Christ, bodily, they will know Christ in you by your love for one anther-- thus the real need to glorify God in our loving one another.
