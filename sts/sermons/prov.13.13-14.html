2. Lack of Wisdom-- one consequence of sin is that it makes us to be fools! We would pass up the treasures of heaven for the twaddle of this world--

We would rather stand in the halls and chit chat, than enter the study room and be taught the things of God in Scripture

We would rather read a horescope, ann landers, op-eds, editorials, than be taught the things of God in scripture

We would rather drink from the fountain of mere mortals-- in our psychological sentimentalism, scientific ramblilngs, scholarly reviews, historical spins, philosophical reminations-- than drink from the fountain of God in Scripture

This is foolish-- not because there is no place for such things, but because these things are not in the same league with scripture...

Result: we hold many secrets, we hear many disclosures, but we haven ot the secrests and disclosure to living when we have not the word of God!

2. Discover the sense of wonder in Preaching-- for no medium is more suitable for hearing the word of God-- if done properly-- preaching is like unwrapping a present-

The word comes to us now wrapped in modern/western language, laced with semantic ribbons, cultural bows, etc.

While we are deeply appreciative of the package-- we ultimately want to get to the real present-- the revelation of God is contained in the scriptures of the Old and New Testament-- as it was original disclosed in its original contexts, literary genre and semantic range--

Therefore, Biblical, expositional preaching is the best means to unwrap the package which contains God's word...

Is. 61:1 "The Spirit of the Lord God is upon Me, Because the Lord has anointed Me To preach good tidings to the poor; He has sent Me to heal the brokenhearted, To proclaim liberty to the captives, And the opening of the prison to those who are bound;

Matt. 4:17 From that time Jesus began to preach and to say, "Repent, for the kingdom of heaven is at hand.''

Acts 10:42 "And He commanded us to preach to the people, and to testify that it is He who was ordained by God to be Judge of the living and the dead.

2Tim. 4:2 Preach the word Be ready in season and out of season. Convince, rebuke, exhort, with all longsuffering and teaching.

By implication-- you should never settle for less than expositional preaching!

Expositional preaching is NOT to be confused with preaching on Bible topics-- it is preaching Biblical topics in scriptural aspects and proportions...

Expositional preaching is NOT to be confused with metephysical discussions on the psychology of religioin or the philosophy of religion, as though this would feed the children of Christ with a sort of processed milk-- it is instead meant to be a bottle filled with the pure milk provided from them by Christ-- with the exact chemical ingredients that Christ himself put into it...

Illus: Difference between wanting real grape juice vs. grape flavored drink

Expositional preaching is NOT to be confused with line by line comentary-- it is instead the careful study of a unit of scriptue as it comes to us as in whole sentences, paragraphs and units

Illus: The difference between eating one vegetable out of vegetable soup-- rather than eating a whole spoon full-- both the taste and nutritional effect is all together different.

Once you have found expositional preaching-- nothing in this world would keep you from it--

I.e. I hope you come to church, not just to be a good model to your children, or to see your friends--
