Text: Pro. 18:20-21

The power of the tongue

Intro:

 

Exposition:

Note: vs. 20 and 21 go together:

Notice that beside the common theme of concerning the tongue both vs. 20-21, vs. 20 begins with "from the fruit of) and vs. 21 ends with "its fruit." Therefore, the two go together!

Both are about the "tongue"

I. vs. 20: Established that the tongue does in fact produce a kind of "fruit" for our edification.

vs. 20: From the fruit of a persons (man's) mouth, ones stomach is sataisfied-- the harvest of the lips brings satisfaction

A. synonomous parrelelism-- the first part restates the meaning of the second

B. Assumes a certain kind of anthropology that... We are not just bodies, but persons with souls such that "words" do in fact feed or poison us-- here the point is to consider "words" as a kind of food for our souls...

It is a simple fact-- that the old saying" sticks and stones may break my bones but words will never hurt me"-- is a lie!

(Kids-- not to say that we should take all words seriously-- many things are said for reasons of insurity, fear, crowd pleasing, etc but who don't really mean what they are saying-- its a sad but constant fact of life such that we should not get our personal worth from what people say-- especially on the school yard)

But-- the fact remains that as living souls, ideas, and especially ideas about ourselves and others-- can have a kind of "feeding" quality such that we are either stronger or weaker for them.

Here-- the emphapsis is positive-- that our words have the power to actually "feed" another persons soul! The metphor of eating is instruduced-- so as to say that one can be satisfied (blessed) by the use of his mouth in communication

II. vs. 21: All Words are not equal

A. vs. 21 reaffirms that words do in fact have power--

Here the expression (in the hand) would mean that something is in the position of the tongue- under the control of the tonge or under its influence and power.

But notice carefully what is under the tongues control!

B. Nothing short of Death and life--

vs. 21: Death and life (are) under the control (hand-- for influence, power) of the tongue

I.e. words can either either take life away from the soul, or they can give life to the soul.

Think about what is being said here-- that words, words from your mouth, words from your pen, words -- can have make or break a person

Whew-- thats scary-- and that's the point!

C. The tongue should be Controled:

Masoretic (NRSV)and those who love it consume its fruit.

LXX: Kratew (difference between bha and nja)

but the ones who keep hold of it eat the fruit of it.

Completes the point that the tongue has power for either good or evil)

Interestingly, the focus here is on our own words effects upon ourselves--

That not only do our words have power to make or break another person, but that our words have power to make or break ourselves...

Deals with which words we consume-- some that will kill us, others that will give life...

This is also the point in

Prov. 13:3 He who guards his mouth preserves his life, But he who opens wide his lips shall have destruction.

Therefore, those who use the tongue wisely will eat its fruit. (as returning to analogy of vs. 20)-- and we have come full circle

Mainpoint: The power of the tongue ought to be carefully managed since it has the power to either make ore break both others and ourselves.

Application:

tongue-- figuratively for communication-- Not just what we say, but what we write, what we publish, etc.

1. Allow your tongue to search your heart.

Jer. 17:9 The heart is deceitful above all things, And desperately wicked; Who can know it?

Luke 6:45 A good man out of the good treasure of his heart brings forth good; and an evil man out of the evil treasure of his heart brings forth evil. For out of the abundance of the heart his mouth speaks.

If we had no malice in our hearts, there would be no malice in our speech. This is why Jesus said: "Love your enemies and pray for those who persecute you."

2. Negative: Control the tongue.

Learn about yourself. When do you use words to injure? When you are:

-tired?

-discouraged?

-frustrated?

-angry?

-insecure?

-on the telephone?

-with certain people?

3. Positive: Cultivate the power of the tongue for good.

a. Encouragement

b. Comfort

c. Instruction

In General, be Imitators of Christ

Eph. 4:31 Let all bitterness, wrath, anger, clamor, and evil speaking be put away from you, with all malice. 32 And be kind to one another, tenderhearted, forgiving one another, just as God in Christ forgave you. 1 Therefore be imitators of God as dear children. 2 And walk in love, as Christ also has loved us and given Himself for us, an offering and a sacrifice to God for a sweet-smelling aroma. 3 But fornication and all uncleanness or covetousness, let it not even be named among you, as is fitting for saints; 4 neither filthiness, nor foolish talking, nor coarse jesting, which are not fitting, but rather giving of thanks.

Don’t make excuses for abusive speech. "He deserved it," or "Well, it’s true."

Our Lord Jesus encountered every sort of person we encounter, and experienced every injustice we experience; yet he never spoke an abusive or malicious word.

But not only should we imitate Christ, we should receive his substituionary words so as to accomplish what our words could not-- the rightous standard of God so as to be admitted into his eternal and saving presence--

It was Chrsit's words, that will ultimately save you-- and here then is the ultimate point of our passage in redemptive history

That the power of the tongue for edification and satisfaction for the soul is ultimately the power of Christ's words! Christ is the word that gives life with the power to do good and not evil.
