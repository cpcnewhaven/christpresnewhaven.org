Prov. 20:20

Wisdom for Children

Introduction: Hey children...

Ordinarily, you children are asked to listen to a sermon that is more or less adult in style and content...

We make no apology for this and in fact believe this to be a good thing.

1. Children, like yourself would have been required to be present when these passages were first read-- as members of the covenant family...

Ex. NT- 1 in every 727 lines are devoted to children--

a. That NT did invision children listening -- since at times it did address children

b. That most of the time, children were not addressed as children-- but using content suitable for adults as well.

Why?

2. It provided them and you with an opportunity to hear what is expected of mature and adult participation in the covenant family-- so that you can grow into maturity...

3. One day, you will want to become an adult-- you will in fact do things to throw off childhood-- it is good for you to grow up seeing that religion isnt' childish, and that when you are ready to become an adult-- you won't discard religion as part of childish thiings (as so many do) but will see a religion that is waiting for you in adult land so to speak...

4. You learn at an early age that worship is not supposed to be fun or entertaining-- where the focus is on your enjoyment rather than on God's pleasure.. Worship may be richly pleasant, proufoundly fulfilling, and lead to unspeakable joy, but in can never never be "fun or "light". It is not a matter of "light fun" to come before the Judge of all things, it isnot a matter of light fun to renew our covenant obligations to God, it is not a matter of light fun to think of God's' Son dying on the cross for our sins...

So it is a good thing that you children are required to attend worship, most of which is not "playful" or childish with child only content...

[Let every parant pick up the paper on the book table and read it concerning why we want children to participate in covenant family worship]

Having said all this, today we come to one of those passages that actually was intended for children-- so children, I am mostly speaking to you today, and just as you are asked to participate in sermons mostly for adults, today we will ask your parents to participate in a sermon mostly for children!

Text:

The one who curses his father and/or mother, his lamp will be put out so as to be in total darkness

Exposition:

Kids, have you ever heard of Pinnochio?

(if you saw the Walt Disney version, you probably didn't get the point of the classical book version)

Marinette-- (talking wood puppet) who wanted to be a real boy really bad-- he was made into a boy like puppet by a very kind man who really wanted to be a father....

Well-- the story is told how Pinnochi almost never became a boy-- do you know why-- because he could obey his father! And this got him into alot of trouble-- it was as if he was living, but without direction or "light" so that he just constantly got into bad trouble--

1. Father said to go to school, but a friend said go to toy land-- to Pinochio was turned into a donkey and learted thaqt is is better to obey his parent than to obey his friends...

2. Another time, his Father told him to go to schoo, but instead he ran away to get rich fast-- but he got robbed and was left to die by the cat and the fox,

I.e. over and over again, Pinochio disobeyed his father, and over and over again, Pinochio got into bad trouble-- but worse still-- Pinochio could become a real boy! Not until finally one day Pinochio learned that it was smarter to obey his parents than to do his own thing-- he began to appreciate the love and wisdom of his father, even searched for his father-- until one day, Pinochio-- the wooden marienette-- became a boy!

Kid's the story of Pinnochio is what this proverb is all about!

This proverb is about what happens to those who don't think its cool to obey parents-- kids who would go

yuck, uuugh, etc. about the 5th commandment

A. Kids-- Do You Remember the 5th Commandment?

Honor your Father and Mother

And it will go well with you...

Do you see how this command has to parts

1. command, honor (obey) your parents...

2. promise of something good if you keep it--it will go well with you-- i.e. you will have a good life...

Well you see that this proverb has two parts also,

And the two parts are very much like the two parts in the 5th commandment, except stated as an obsersavation based upon the 5th commandment.

Observation-- means that the wise man figured something out from practical experience! If you live long enough, you begin to notice some things!

What he found out was that those who violated the 5th, who didn't honor and obey their parents-- well bad things seems always to happen to them...

Just like Pinochio?

Point out three things:

1. father and mother-- Concerns both how your treat your mother and father-- they are both equally deserving of your repect and obedience!

God authorizes parents to represent him to dependents-- so to curse parents is to curse God, and to distrust God, so then yo expect punishment by God...

Lev. 20:9 All who curse father or mother shall be put to death; having cursed father or mother, their blood is upon them.

2. "Curse"-- To curse a parent is the opposite of "honor" and "respect"

1. scoff-- stomps, growls, grumbles---

2. be desrespectful-- yea,

3. think lightly of what they say--- "okay mom"

4. Disobey-- when they tell you to do or not do something-- and you sneak it in anyway-- and then say something like "I forgot" or "I slipped", or "Huh? (with that funny looking face)

5. Proud/rebellious--

Prov. 30:11 There is a generation that curses its father, And does not bless its mother. 12 There is a generation that is pure in its own eyes, Yet is not washed from its filthiness. 13 There is a generation--oh, how lofty are their eyes And their eyelids are lifted up.

I guess it should not surprize you, as you have been taught-- that many are running away from God-- and so run away from God's commandments--

As a child-- no where will you see this more than in the area of dishonoring parents-- (since at the heart of all sin, is rebellion against authority-- god's authority and those who represent God's authority-- for children that is mostly parents)

3. What happens to those who curse (dishonor and disobey) thier parents

his lamp will be put out so as to be in total darkness

I.e.. bad things will happen!

I.e. just as if you honor your parents life will go well for you (live with light), if you curse your parents life will go bad (total darkness)

Words used can refer also to a night without a moon--

Have you ever tried to walk through the woods or even in a room that was totally dark-- what will likely happen--- bump into things, trip, fall, etc.

This is what happens when you try to live your life without respecting your parents...

You go through life bumping into things that you were not able to see because you cursed your parents..

Illustration: Next time you go camping, put out the flashlight if there isn't a moon-- see what happens-- then remember this proverb

Mainpoint: This proverb teaches you that you want to never curses your parents, but rather honor and obey them or else you will be like the person walking in darkness-- it will not be a good walk!

Other proverbs:

Prov. 1:8Hear, my child, your father's instruction, and do not reject your mother's teaching; 9 for they are a fair garland for your head, and pendants for your neck.

Prov. 4:1 Listen, children, to a father's instruction, and be attentive, that you may gain insight;

Prov. 15:5 A fool despises a parent's instruction, but the one who heeds admonition is prudent.

Application:

1. Children: Think of ways you can become not less respectful, but more respectful to your parents...

a. How do you respond when your parents ask you to do something? (as if they are doing you a favor to give you a flashlight in darkness, or as if they are doing something bad to you) Say-- sure! or "yes sir, yes mam"

b. How do you obey-- what kind of attitdue do you have? with out scoffing, whinning, complaining, but happily because you know your parents are like your flashlight in a dark night.

c. Never never sneak!, lie! or hide disobedience! You might get turned into a donkey (just kidding-- but you may find that you will feel like a donkey)

Just remember-- those who are running away from God will try to make it seem "cute" or "funny" or even "cool" to not honor and respect their parents-- you are not to be like that! F0r you know that the kid who curses his parents is like the person without a light in the pitch black dark!

 

2. Parents- Think of ways you can make it easier and not harder for your children to obey

a. Make sure you make demands that are worthy demands..

(Distinguish between personality and morality-- Biblical values and personal preferences!)

Cloths, Hair style, etc-- are they things that are genuinely moral or life effecting...

Many judgment calls and you ahve the right to make them, but if children get the idea that it is a arbitrary excercise of authority, they will be more tempted to rebel.

b.Help convince you children that it really is to their best interest to obey you-- this is the motivation that is stated in this proverb

1) as children get older, it will help more to explain things to them

2) keep their emotional tank full-- by spending time enjoying them-- kids associate love with play-- they will feel loved if you do in fact play with them etc..

c. Have a child friendly home but not a child driven home--

a) ON the one hand, you will want to make sure that your home doesn't feel like a place where your children are entruding on you! They should feel that thier being in your home is good-- and not somewho destructive to your goals!

Think about the things you feel strongly about in your home-- are they things that are rooted in biblical values and are they things that make allowances for children growing up...

b) On the other hand, a child friendly home is not a child driven home-- they are happier with firm/loving discipline-- you are a light to them-- shine that light of guidance, help, etc.

Don't pass off your responsibility with under the disquise of -- the sooner they learn to do things on their own the better--

Its true that eventually your children will need to be lovingly "pushed" to do things for themselves-- but only after years of loving guidance and careful attention to training!

Example: children might not be the best at ordering their time-- you determine bed time, study time, worship times, what things to train in, etc.

I.e. You will be their light as to when and how to use their time-- whether learning catechisms or playing a video game, just as you do for worldly tings.

d. children who pray with parents are more likely to obey parents---

When children hear parents in family worship prayer for their well being and godlliness-- they will be more prone to honor those parents...

When children have parents to pray with them about things that are happening in their life, they will trust parants more to lead them in life-- knowing their parents are follwing God

e. Parents who themselves "honor" and respect authority are more likely to have children who live under authority with respect.

Do they see you live with submissive attitudes and respect toward those in authority over you.

Mothers-- do you model respect by your treatment of your husband.

Husband, do you model respect by your treatment of your session, and ultimately of Christ

Conclusion: Children, do you perfectly honor and respect your parents-- parents, do you perfectly honor and respect your authorities?

Well like all teachings derived from God's law-- this provebr shows why we needed a savior!

Left to our own merits, we could never gain the blessing of God's covenant into eternal life! Which is why Christ came-- to complete what we couldn't!

Kids-- while you should seek to obey your parents-- every time you have that bad attitude-- you should also remember Jesus-- for at that moment you see why Jesus came-- to save you from the penalty of your sin---

His was a life of perfect respect and honor of his heavenly father--

One aspect of his humiliation is that he was n fact "born under the law"

Christ could say in John 8:49 I honor My Father,

And We know the greatest honor that Christ gave to the father was his obedience, even to death on the cross

So as you look at this table-- look at Christ who was obedient to his father.

Therefore-- as you look at this table, and when you put your faith in Jesus-- remember that Jesus became your substitute-- he obeyed the father perfectly-- so that his obedience is counted as your obedience-- and your hope then for eternal life is not on yourself and your ability to obey your parents-- it is on Christ and his ability to obey his parent- his father---

Let us all then come with thanksgiving, that our salvation does not rest upon ourselves, but on Christ!
