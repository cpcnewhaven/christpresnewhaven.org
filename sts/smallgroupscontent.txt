  Groups at CPC </a></h2>
  <p class=MsoNormal align=center style='mso-margin-top-alt:auto;mso-margin-bottom-alt:
  auto;text-align:center'><span style='mso-bookmark:cpctop'>(For upcoming
  meeting times, please consult the </span>Small Group Fellowship Information
  located in the <a
  href="javascript:parent.location='../oth/bulletinannouncements.htm'">Bulletin
  Announcements</a>.) </p>
  <p>Another and very important way that you can get involved in “fellowship”
  at CPC is to participate in a fellowship group. A fellowship group is
  structured to be “the church in miniature,” a kind of “house church” albeit
  informally expressed within closer geographical proximity to where a person
  lives. The purpose of the fellowship group is to complement the one <span
  class=SpellE><span class=spelle>anothering</span></span> we experience
  corporately as a whole in a more personal and more readily available way.
  Toward this end, there is most likely a fellowship group in your neighborhood!
  Each group will participate together in informal teaching, worship, sharing,
  prayer and in the provision of mutual help for one another in times of need.
  Each group is assigned a trained “facilitator,” mercy coordinator,
  host/hostess and worship leader. We strongly encourage everyone in the church
  to participate in a “fellowship” group in order to experience the life of God
  as expressed more locally and intimately through a small group context! </p>
