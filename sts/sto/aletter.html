November 20, 1996

Dear Friends of Christ Presbyterian Church,

It has been some time since I have been able to communicate with you-- and for this I am genuinely sorry. I honestly can't remember a time in my life or in the life of this church when ministry was more exciting but also more hectic. I have learned in ministry to think "seasonally"-- this has surely been a productive but busy season.. As you know, my ordinary practice in correspondence has been to tell the stories of people being changed by the power of the gospel through Christ Presbyterian Church. I'm excited to report that I could readily relate many such exciting stories at this time--

For instance, we have witnessed several new conversions over the past several months. We have also started several new "Discovery groups" targeting unchurched people (one group of Ghanaians and Kenyans, another group of graduate students from Singapore, and another community study in a southern section of the Greater New Haven region. Please pray for the unchurched people from around the world who are being led to the gospel through these groups. Two of these groups are being led by our student interns, one from Ghana and the other two from Singapore, who are being trained for church leadership and perhaps the core team of a church plant in their home countries. These groups alone would make for many wonderful stories of the exciting international and regional outreach God has brought to our church. It should really make us stop and think that through this little church, we have the very real opportunity of planting a church in Ghana, Singapore and Southern CT in the not so distant future. And just last night, two people expressed a desire to surrender their lives to Christ in the Southern CT discover group!

Moreover, we are very encouraged to have a good spirit of peace and purity in our congregation. Our Sunday service is jammed packed with people (avg. about 165 to 170 in a space that is meant to seat about 120 comfortably)-- which of course is great, but has presented us with the real challenge of finding a new place to meet lest we keep hitting the "ceiling" and put a lid on our momentum. Yet herein lies the challenge-- whereas we have a congregation that looks 170 strong, in financial terms we are really a congregation with far less people who are able to give. As you can see, this presents a kind of "catch 22."

In response to these wonderful challenges, our session concluded last week to take a carefully calculated but bold step for the future viability of this church into the next century. Having located a site that is strategically perfect for the vision of our church of reaching the Yale/New Haven community, and having completed the process toward building congregational consensus (a unanimous vote at yesterdays congregational meeting), the session agreed to pursue the purchase of a new facility. The selected site would place us in a high visibility location within an easy walking distance of the Yale community, but also within a block of the two interstates that connect us to the surrounding edge cities of New Haven. We can feel the gravity of all this when we consider that what we do today will impact the future vision and viability of CPC well into the next century. When it comes to "vision", location is everything-- which is why we feel so blessed that a site like this has come available giving us the perfect location to reach the Yale community and the nations represented here.

The plan is for the project to be accomplished in two stages. The first stage will be to secure the property which presently has a very attractive historical building that would function to house our study center and offices. Stage 2 would be to build a suitable worship center on the property that would take us well into the next century. Until phase 2 is completed, we will need to go to two services in our present location so as to sustain growth.

In order for this vision to become a reality-- we will need to find money that has up to this time been unavailable. On the front end, we will only need to raise $50,000 by closing to get into phase one. In order to begin phase 2, we will need to raise an additional $400,000 either through one-time donations or through a pledged donation that could be executed over a period of 2 to 3 years. (Of course the more we raise the less our debt and the better our facility.) These raised amounts also assumes a relationship with the "Investors Fund"-- a unique lending agency for churches in our denomination that allows for some creative options toward reducing the debt burden.

We will be asking ourselves and even people who are not currently familiar with the vision of CPC to sincerely consider what could be a better use of assets than to help build a study and worship center that will literally reach and train many people from around the world in Christian faith and practice for the glory of Christ. It's money given to a tangible site and location that will have, in the words of the apostle Paul, "far, wide and deep" spiritual influences. We are quite simply asking God to do something tangible that will forever remind all who know this church of His blessings and commitment to a vision such as will promote Christ as having first place in everything. We want to be compelled by a sincere devotion to Christ. And we believe that it was Christ that gave our church this strategic mission and vision in the Yale/New Haven community.

For now, you can pray with us that a deal will do through. Throughout this process, we have prayed that God would lead us in both wisdom and faith for the sake of His glory. Hopefully we will be in touch soon about some of the ways you may want to get involved.

And as you can see, we are at one of the most significant and strategic moments ever toward the accomplishment of an ambitious vision for the glory of Christ. What we do now will secure a better future for our witness in this strategic community for years to come-- surely to exceed our own time here on earth. How many people's lives from around the world will be changed? How many people transformed into Christian leaders? Who knows, how many world leaders will learn the way of discipleship from this church that will change the course of history-- all at least partially because of the decisions we are making at this time. One thing is for sure, while no "house" can be built without the continued power of God, having a tangible and established "house" will go a long way toward making our long term vision a reality here! We are helping to secure the future of what we hold to be a Godly heritage-- a continuing tradition making history in New Haven!

We are looking to God for some amazing things here. And again, your prayers and gifts are never taken for granted. Thanks! And please be encouraged as your labors and gifts have not been in vain, but are every day being multiplied for the Kingdom of God throughout the world!

I am affectionately yours,
Preston
