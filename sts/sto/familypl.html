Developing a Plan For A Stronger Family

Everyone seems to be talking about the American family these days-- both on the political right and left. And whereas there are different definitions of what a family is, there is an amazing degree of consensus that the future of our society is as good or bad as the future of our families. And without a doubt, the present "breakdown" of functioning families has clearly been one of the greatest hurtles we have faced in building an affective church in the greater New Haven region. Of particular concern has been the lack of family involvement by many of the husbands represented by wives and children attending our church. This is why I was especially delighted to see Mark, Greg and Michael walk into the conference room this past Saturday for a day long seminar on Developing a Plan for a Stronger Family.

I had previously met with each of these men individually regarding various family matters and their status with God. Each were unreserved in communicating their inability to believe in Christ. Each would also agree that something was not right about their lives and families. Hmmm! The seminar was led from the perspective of constructing a strategic plan whereby Biblical values might be transformed into a vision and corresponding goals toward the construction of a healthy family. Everyone, especially the men, seemed to appreciate this "management" approach to thinking about the family. As the seminar progressed, it became evident that the kind of values that make for healthy families are directly related to a personal relationship with Christ. Whereas healthy families was not presented as the ultimate goal of life (this being reserved to our being restored in our relation with God and giving him glory), having a relationship with Christ was clearly shown to be relevant to having a healthy home. How else can we forgive, lest we have know forgiveness from God? How else can we put others interests before our own, lest we have first been loved by God in this way? And how else will we be able to foster Christian love, joy, peace, patience, kindness, goodness and self control, lest we have such a spirit living within us? My hope is that one more barrier separating these men from an eternal relationship with Christ may have been lowered as they associated with other Christian men and discovered the plausibility of Christian faith-- at least in regards to the home.

This past Saturday represented our annual church plan to target issues related to the family in various special events. We have a great need to reach families, and to build up the families that we have. I led a seminar on the Christian discipleship of Children last month. This month I will lead another seminar on "Finding a Spouse" whereby Biblical goals and principles concerning marriage will be outlined. Please continue your support and prayers for our church as we attempt to reach this very strategic region with a viable Christian faith!
