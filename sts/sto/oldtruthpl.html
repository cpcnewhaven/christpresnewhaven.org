An Old Truth Brought Home Again

There are times when old truths are said in just the right way so as to have a particularly useful affect in life. Such a time was today in reading John Flavel's masterful work, The Fountain of Life. In meditating upon Christ's mediatorial office as King, Flavel adds,

then do you also leave all your particular concerns in the hands of Christ too, and know that the infinite wisdom and love, which rules the world, manages every thing that relates to you?"


Thump! I knew that God was speaking to me. Would I leave my concern for this church in the hands of Christ? Could I believe that in relocating one of our most significant families to Washington DC., Christ's infinite love and wisdom were both operative? Could I trust in God's plan to take away one of our desperately needed elders? Or would I repine and fret at providence only for it's crossing my own will and wisdom? Indeed, this has been a difficult month. While we have seen God do many wonderful things-- two conversions, our services filled with people and many other wonderful evidences of God's blessings here, my thoughts have too much been on our great need for healthy, mature families-- only to see one of our most ministry minded families relocated. At no surprise to me, God obviously has planned to pastor the pastor.

As for our church, it is both a concern and a privilege that we have so many students, singles and spiritually widowed spouses attending our church. As I am often reminded, we don't live on Walton's mountain, at least not here in New Haven. But please do pray that God would bring to us people and families that might assist us in our mission to make disciples in New Haven. Having lost one of our elders, we are in particular need for God to raise up other leaders-- even more so because of the growing number of people attending our Sunday services.

One of the more special opportunities for our church is to develop strategies for helping our people think Christianly about modern life. Last month, we sponsored a lecture and discussion entitled "Gender Wars" which was well attended. This month, the men have been meeting every Wednesday morning at 6:30 am to discuss "Biblical Manhood."

We are so grateful to each of you for the ministry you have here through giving. Since our church caters to so many University people, your gifts are particularly useful in sustaining Christ Presbyterian Church, New Haven. Please also continue to pray for God's grace in our lives to do His will with joy!
