Significant and Pivotal Year

In my previous work with a para-church college ministry, the national director once introduced his New Year address with "this will be a significant and pivotal year in the life of..." When the following year's address was again introduced with the exact same words, and then the next year, and the next-- well, you get the picture. It seems that at the beginning of every year, we recognize that something special could happen. Hind-sight tells us that in reality, only a few years over the span of our whole lifetime are really "significant and pivotal years." So I guess that if I tell you that 1996 could be a "significant and pivotal year," in the life of Christ Presbyterian Church, you will most likely be thinking "sure." But all the same, let me try to convince you if per chance I can also persuade you to commit yourself to prayer on our behalf.

First, this is a year that we hope will result in our finding a more permanent facility. That this needs to happen is easy to defend. That this indeed will happen depends on a miracle. As to the need, we find that our present location is perfectly located but without the potential for further growth. As to the means, since so many of our people live under the poverty line as undergrad and graduate students, our numerical growth is disproportionate to our financial growth. The bottom line is that for us to have a facility, we will need to discover outside funding in a significant way. The pay off to having a facility would be, 1) increased space for growth 2) a stable presence in New Haven to help compensate for what is otherwise a transient congregation of people.

Second, one of the greatest privileges for a church like ours is the potential leadership development of people who will be trained to serve the church in leadership roles throughout the world. But such a mission presupposes a solid leadership base from which to stabilize our ministry. This is why at the present, it is so hard to lose our leadership families. And since this is not a high growth area for stable jobs, we stand to lose more to relocation than we stand to gain through the long process of training those who do trickle into our region This year, like last year, we stand to lose one of our key leadership families due to relocation. It took just about the whole two year time of their being with us for them to emerge as leaders in our church. This year could go either way in terms of our leadership development. Please pray that God would provide a larger pool of stable leadership for our church.

These are just two of the reasons why 1996 could be "a very significant and pivotal year" for Christ Presbyterian Church, New Haven. Your prayers and continued support are deeply appreciated.
