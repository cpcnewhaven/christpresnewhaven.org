<P>

Christ Presbyterian Church is a congregation of the Presbyterian Church in America (PCA), a growing denomination committed to the historic Christian faith and to biblical values and mission.  We desire our ministry to be consistent with the well established principles of the classic Reformed faith while also being relevant to modern life.  We desire to be an accepting, unpretentious people who experience God's grace through Christ, even as we seek to extend God's grace through church planting in New England and throughout the world.
</P>
